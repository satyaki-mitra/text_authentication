Moreover, our independent review of the entire statute confirms that nowhere does it confer such an unconditional right of intervention. Since “[t]he burden of proof in an intervention action [under Rule 52.12(a)] lies with the proposed intervenor,” In re Estate of Potashnick, 841 S.W.2d 714, 719 (Mo.App. E.D.1992), the trial court properly overruled SVC’s motion to intervene on the basis of Rule 52.12(a)(1).
