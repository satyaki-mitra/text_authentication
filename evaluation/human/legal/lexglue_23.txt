Further, the JCC found that Claimant reached maximum medical improvement on July 1, 2002; therefore, it was error to determine that Claimant was entitled to TTD benefits following attainment of maximum medical improvement. Under the plain language of section 440.15(2)(a), TTD benefits cease when the employee reaches maximum medical improvement. We find, however, that because Claimant was working a reduced number of hours, he may be entitled to temporary partial disability (TPD) benefits for the period before reaching maximum medical improvement.
