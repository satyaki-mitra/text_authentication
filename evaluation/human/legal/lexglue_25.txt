Moreover, the district court gave Rackstraw a two-level reduction for being a minor participant under U.S.S.G. § 3B1.2(b) and sentenced him at the bottom of the applicable guideline range. Any disparity between the sentence imposed on Green and that imposed on Rack-straw is easily explained by the fact that Green cooperated with the government in the prosecution of participants in William and Fisher’s drug operations, while Rackstraw did not.
