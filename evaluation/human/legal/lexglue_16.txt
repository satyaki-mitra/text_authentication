In addition, the Supreme Court of Texas and this court have concluded that a parent’s duty of support includes a duty to pay for the necessaries of the parent’s children. See In re H.V., 252 S.W.3d at 327, n. 55; In re W.J.S, 35 S.W.3d at 277-278. Under section 151.001(c) and this court’s opinion in Hardin, a trial court’s finding that attorney’s fees are necessaries for the benefit of the child supports a trial court’s order that a parent pay these fees as additional child support.
