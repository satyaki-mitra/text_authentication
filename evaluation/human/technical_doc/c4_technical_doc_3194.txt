Lalie is a cute caterpillar and she wants to help kids discover the world. Lalie Christmas will introduce them to magical world of Christmas. Kids will love to scratch 30 different shapes, hear their names and move them on the screen to create their own arrangements. And everything while listening Christmas Carols. With Lalie your children will develop their imagination, manual skills and attention to details. They will also learn their first words in other languages! Follow us on Twitter: twitter.com/laliegames or like us on Facebook: facebook.com/laliegames for more updates and fun! •30 different shapes of toys, gifts and Christmas ornaments. •3 languages: English, Spanish and French. •“Merry Christmas”, “Jingle Bells” and “O Holy Night” Christmas Carols as orchestrated by Singing Bell with their permission. •No rules, just scratch to discover the toys, gifts and Christmas ornaments and move them freely. •Take photos of your creations.
