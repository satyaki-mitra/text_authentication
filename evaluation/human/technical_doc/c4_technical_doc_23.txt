Medical Society of the County of Monroe, Inc. Subject files of committees, minutes of meetings, reports, records of death associated with pregnancy and childbirth, constitution and bylaws as amended June 1876, financial records, pamphlets, and scrapbooks of the Medical Society of the County of Monroe, Inc. The Medical Society of the County of Monroe was formed in 1821. Records open only to qualified researchers approved by the President of the Board of Directors or the Executive Director of the Medical Society of the County of Monroe, Inc. Dates in the guide below may be circa and not absolute in reference to the subject files. Medical Society of the County of Monroe records, #3083. Division of Rare and Manuscript Collections, Cornell University Library.
