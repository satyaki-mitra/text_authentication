New Hours Night, presented in the current 2012 the Swiss watch company Dietrich, created for romantics, lovers in the night. On a matte black surface like a clock face dissolve into the dark central hour and minute hands, executed in rich-black tone. Indicator small seconds hand at 6 o’clock position has two options in the performance: steel and black. Novelty has a classic round stainless steel case back is made sapphire glass. On the front of the building is also equipped with a sapphire crystal with anti-reflective coating. Fluted crown on the body is placed in position for 3 hours. New Night at the Swiss mechanism operates ETA 6498-1 manual winding. To comply with the overall tone of the black, the mechanism of rhodium is fully dressed in black finish. New Night released in five versions: stainless steel with black PVD-coated or not, face different colors – dark blue or black, arrows coated with varnish dark blue or black shades, and a model with an additional small second dial arrow. New Night comes on alligator strap in black and equipped with a steel buckle. Each model included a black travel bag.
