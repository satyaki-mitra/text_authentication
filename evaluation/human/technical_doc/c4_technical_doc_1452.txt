We invite applications by Tuesday 7th April 2015 for a fully-funded, three-year PhD studentship at the Angus McIntosh Centre for Historical Linguistics, starting September 2015 (or very shortly thereafter). The successful applicant’s primary research focus is expected to be on any aspect of historical linguistics that is within the expertise of the staff of Linguistics and English Language at Edinburgh. This studentship is funded by the Angus McIntosh Centre for Historical Linguistics. The award provides for full tuition fees (UK, EU or Overseas), and an annual stipend of £14,000 for three years. Existing doctoral researchers (ie. those in their 1st or 2nd year of doctoral study) are not eligible for this award. Complete the studentship application form available on the Linguistics and English Language website and submit with the required supporting documentation by Tuesday 7th April 2015. Interviews (by Skype if necessary) will take place in mid-April 2015.
