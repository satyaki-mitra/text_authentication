we derive a semiclassical time evolution kernel and a trace formula for the dirac equation . the classical trajectories that enter the expressions are determined by the dynamics of relativistic point particles . we carefully investigate the transport of the spin degrees of freedom along the trajectories which can be understood geometrically as parallel transport in a vector bundle with su(2 ) holonomy . furthermore , we give an interpretation in terms of a classical spin vector that is transported along the trajectories and whose dynamics , dictated by the equation of thomas precession , gives rise to dynamical and geometric phases every orbit is weighted by . we also present an analogous approach to the pauli equation which we analyse in two different limits . ulm - tp/98 - 6 + november 1998 + * a semiclassical approach to the + dirac equation * + and stefan keppeler + abteilung theoretische physik + universitt ulm , albert - einstein - allee 11 + d-89069 ulm , germany
