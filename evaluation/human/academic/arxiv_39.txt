we consider a dilute gas of dipole moments in an arbitrary harmonic trap and treat both the short - range , isotropic delta - interaction and the long - range , anisotropic dipole - dipole interaction perturbatively . with this we calculate the leading shift of the critical temperature with respect to that of an ideal gas as a function of the relative orientation of the dipole moments with respect to the harmonic trap axes . in particular , we determine those magic angles , where the dipolar shift of the bose - einstein condensation temperature vanishes . furthermore , we show for the parameters of the ongoing @xmath0cr - experiment in stuttgart that this dipolar shift can be enhanced by increasing the number of particles , the geometrical mean trap frequency , and the anisotropy of the trap .
