we investigate the origin of the ubiquitous 0.5 10 hz qpo in the galactic microquasar grs 1915 + 105 . using the archival x - ray data from rxte , we make a wide band x - ray spectral fitting to the source during a low - hard state observed in 1999 june . we resolve the x - ray spectra into three components , namely a multi - color disk component , a comptonised component and a power - law at higher energies . this spectral description is favored compared to other normally used spectra like a cut - off power law , hard components with reflection etc . we find that the 0.5 10 hz qpo is predominantly due to variations in the comptonised component . we use this result to constrain the location of the various spectral components in the source . = cmbx8 = cmmi9 = cmss9 = cmti8.tfm
