we study a holomorphic representation for spinfoams . the representation is obtained via the ashtekar - lewandowski - marolf - mouro - thiemann coherent state transform . we derive the expression of the 4d spinfoam vertex for euclidean and for lorentzian gravity in the holomorphic representation . the advantage of this representation rests on the fact that the variables used have a clear interpretation in terms of a classical intrinsic and extrinsic geometry of space . we show how the peakedness on the extrinsic geometry selects a single exponential of the regge action in the semiclassical large - scale asymptotics of the spinfoam vertex .
