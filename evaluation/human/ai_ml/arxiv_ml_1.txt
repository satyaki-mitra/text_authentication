the emergence and magnetic evolution of solar active regions ( ars ) of @xmath0-type , which are known to be highly flare - productive , were studied with the soho / mdi data in cycle 23 . we selected 31 ars that can be observed from their birth phase , as unbiased samples for our study . from the analysis of the magnetic topology ( twist and writhe ) , we obtained the following results . 1 ) emerging @xmath0 ars can be classified into three topological types as `` quasi-@xmath1 '' , `` writhed '' and `` top - to - top '' . 2 ) among them , the `` writhed '' and `` top - to - top '' types tend to show high flare activity . 3 ) as the signs of twist and writhe agree with each other in most cases of the `` writhed '' type ( 12 cases out of 13 ) , we propose a magnetic model in which the emerging flux regions in a @xmath0 ar are not separated but united as a single structure below the solar surface . 4 ) almost all the `` writhed''-type ars have downward knotted structures in the mid portion of the magnetic flux tube . this , we believe , is the essential property of @xmath0 ars . 5 ) the flare activity of @xmath0 ars is highly correlated not only with the sunspot area but also with the magnetic complexity . 6 ) we suggest that there is a possible scaling - law between the flare index and the maximum umbral area .
