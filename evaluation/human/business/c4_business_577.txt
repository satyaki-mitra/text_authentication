Home Dublin Life Dog of the Week Dog of the Week: Meet Isaac! Dog of the Week: Meet Isaac! Dublin Gazette Newspapers has teamed up with Dogs Trust to help find homes for lost and abandoned dogs. Isaac is a stunning two-year-old Collie cross who is looking for a patient and loving family. He is a sensitive soul and can be shy initially, but having the company of another canine friend helps him. Isaac will need to live with another confident dog that will help him settle in. With the company of another pooch pal, Isaac should be fine left alone for a few hours throughout the day. He is a beautiful dog with a heart of gold and will blossom in the right environment – all he needs is somebody to give him a second chance in a loving and understanding home. If you have room in your heart and home for Isaac then please contact Dogs Trust on 01 879 1000. They are based in Finglas, just off exit 5 on the M50.
