Japanese translation offered for all regularly scheduled services. Yokohama International Baptist Church (YIBC) Welcomes you! "A Church for the nations," YIBC is located in Yokohama, Japan, near Negishi Park. People from all nationalities are warmly welcomed. English is our common international language in worship services. We have ministries in English, Japanese, and Tagalog. Our mission is to glorify God by reaching out to the international community and Japanese, to share the love and good news of Jesus. Our vision is to share the good news in every language spoken in Yokohama with every people group of our great international city, and equip every member of our congregation to witness, worship, fellowship, grow in Christ, and minister in a way that “multiplies disciples and Churches among all the nations" until our Lord's return. Our supreme passion is to know Christ personally, and make Him known in all that we do. We are affiliated with the Hawaii Pacific Baptist Convention and welcome believers from all denominations and traditions to fellowship with us at YIBC. Won't you come visit?
