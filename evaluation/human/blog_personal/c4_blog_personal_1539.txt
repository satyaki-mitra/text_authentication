I love it when we receive the images for a property, which we then collate to create a brochure. When I am in initial contact with the vendor over the phone, I haven’t actually seen the house, so it’s nice to see what it looks like! Personally I love all of the pretty cottages we have sold, because they have bags of character and they’re the type of house I would buy myself. Becoming integrated into the team – everyone is so friendly and helpful in regards to both work and personal life. The customer service is key. Everyone in the office builds very good, personal relationships with all the vendors, buyers, landlords and tenants to ensure we deliver the best service. We genuinely care about each individual!
