Title: A Step-by-Step Tutorial for Novices: Installing WordPress on a Local Development Environment Utilizing MAMP (Mac, Apache, MySQL, PHP)

This tutorial will guide you through the process of setting up WordPress on your local development environment by using MAMP. Upon completion, you'll have a functional WordPress installation that serves as a platform for practice and experimentation without affecting your live site.

**Preliminary Requirements:**
1. A Mac computer operating macOS.
2. Obtain MAMP from its [official website](https://www.mamp.info/en/downloads/) (free trial accessible).

**Step 1: Install MAMP Procedure**
   - Locate and double-click the downloaded .dmg file to initiate the installation process.
   - Adhere to the on-screen guidelines to complete the setup. Upon installation, MAMP will be stored in your Applications folder.

**Step 2: Launch and Start Server**
   - Traverse to your Applications folder and launch the MAMP application.
   - Click the "Start Servers" button at the window's bottom to activate Apache and MySQL servers.
   - Monitor the Mac menu bar for a small icon; ensure both Apache and MySQL are active by checking their respective icons.

**Step 3: Database Creation**
   - Inside the MAMP application, access the "PHPMyAdmin" tab.
   - From the left sidebar, click "Database," then press the "Create new database" button.
   - Designate a name for your database (e.g., wordpress) and confirm by clicking "Create." Take note of the chosen name as it will be needed later.

**Step 4: Acquire WordPress**
   - Navigate to the [WordPress download page](https://wordpress.org/download/) to obtain the most recent version of WordPress.
   - Extract the downloaded .zip file. A folder named "wordpress" should appear.
