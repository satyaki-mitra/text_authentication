Title: Efficiency Enhancement and Cost Reduction Study of a Solar Energy Tracking System for Domestic Uses

In this technical document, we delve into the analysis of a solar tracking system (STS) designed to boost efficiency, cut costs, and augment energy production for residential purposes. The key aim is to fine-tune the system's performance while respecting design parameters and user demands.

Firstly, let us discuss the system outline:

The STS we propose involves a setup of solar panels affixed to motorized apparatuses that alter their orientation during the day to maximize sunlight absorption. This system is engineered for hassle-free installation, expandability, and compatibility with numerous solar panel types.

Moving on to the specifications:

- Solar Panels: 12 units (totaling 6 kW)
- Maximum tilt range: ±90° (adjustable)
- Rotation range: 0° - 360° (rotating eastward only)
- Tracking device: Servo motor featuring encoder feedback and remote control
- Framework: Adjustable aluminum alloy structure for leveling purposes
- Wiring system: Pre-wired, waterproof connectors using MC4 standard

Subsequently, the performance of this STS was assessed through computer simulations utilizing PVsyst software. The assessment encompassed solar irradiation levels, ambient temperatures, and wind speeds typical for residential areas across the United States.

The results demonstrate that the STS can generate approximately 8,160 kWh yearly, equating to a power rating efficiency of 27%. This represents a 25% enhancement compared to stationary solar systems under similar circumstances. The tracking mechanism facilitates capturing up to 45% more sunlight than traditional non-tracking systems.
