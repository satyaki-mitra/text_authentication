Title: Data Handling Protocol - Excerpt from Contractual Addendum

I. Establishment and Parties Involved

1.1 This Data Handling Protocol ("Agreement") is executed by [Data Controller Name] ("Entity A") and [Data Processor Name] ("Entity B"), each referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

1.2 The Parties have consented to this Agreement, which serves as an essential component of the primary contract(s) through which Entity B offers services to Entity A (the "Agreement"). This Protocol shall take effect starting on [Effective Date].

II. Purpose and Application

2.1 The objective of this Protocol is to fulfill the Parties' obligations, as stipulated by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), in relation to the management of Personal Data by Entity B on behalf of Entity A.

III. Terminology and Interpretation

3.1 Terms used within this Protocol retain their meanings as defined under GDPR, unless specified otherwise herein. Furthermore, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

(a) "Data Breach" refers to an unauthorized or accidental incident resulting in the destruction, loss, modification, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data during transmission, storage, or processing.

IV. Processor's Responsibilities

4.1 Entity B shall:

(a) process Personal Data only upon receiving documented instructions from Entity A, unless legally obligated by Union or Member State law to which Entity B is subject; in such cases, Entity B will notify Entity A of the applicable legal requirement prior to processing, unless the law explicitly forbids such disclosure on grounds of public interest.

(b) ensure that all individuals authorized to handle Personal Data have agreed to maintain confidentiality or are bound by a statutory duty of secrecy.

(c) implement suitable technical and organizational measures designed to provide an adequate level of security based on the associated risks, such measures may include but are not limited to: data pseudonymization, encryption, ensuring system and service confidentiality, integrity, availability, resilience; restoring access and availability of Personal Data in a timely manner following a physical or technical incident; regular testing, evaluation, and assessment of the effectiveness of these security measures.
