Title: Privacy Protection Policy Declaration

A. Preamble

   A.1 This Privacy Protection Policy Declaration ("Declaration") signifies the Company's ("Entity" or "Data Guardian") dedication towards safeguarding personal information in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation) and any other pertinent privacy protection regulations ("Privacy Laws").

   A.2 The Declaration is applicable to all data processing operations executed by the Entity, its employees, temporary workers, contractors, agents, advisors, or any third parties performing duties on behalf of the Entity ("Data Handlers").

B. Privacy Protection Principles

   B.1 The Entity shall adhere to the following privacy protection principles:

       B.1.1 Lawful, Equitable, and Transparent Data Processing: Personal information will be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently in relation to the data subject.

       B.1.2 Purpose Specification: Personal information shall be gathered for predefined, explicit, and legitimate purposes and should not be further processed in a manner inconsistent with these objectives.

       B.1.3 Data Adequacy: Personal information shall be relevant, pertinent, and limited to the minimum necessary to achieve the intended purposes.

       B.1.4 Accuracy: The Entity will make reasonable efforts to ensure that personal information is accurate and, when required, up-to-date.

       B.1.5 Storage Limitations: Personal information should not be retained in a form that allows identification of data subjects for longer than necessary for the purposes for which the data was collected.

       B.1.6 Data Integrity and Confidentiality: The Entity will implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure that personal information is processed securely, including protection against unauthorized or illegal processing and against accidental loss, destruction, or damage.

       C.1.2 The right of access;

       C.1.3 The right to rectification;
