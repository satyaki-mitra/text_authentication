Title: Assessing the Growth Potential of an Eco-Friendly Product Line Extension for Our Company

Introduction:

In the rapidly changing and increasingly environmentally aware global market, it is essential for our company to keep evolving and adjusting to shifting consumer tastes. This assessment aims to explore the expansion of our product line to incorporate eco-friendly alternatives, aligning with the increasing demand for sustainable products.

Market Expansion: Over the past ten years, the global market for eco-friendly goods has witnessed substantial growth, with a projected compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 15% from 2016 to 2023 (as per Allied Market Research).
