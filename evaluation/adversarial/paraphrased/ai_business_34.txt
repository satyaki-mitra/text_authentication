2. Market Analysis:

A. Market Overview:
The worldwide packaging industry is anticipated to expand at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 4.3% from 2019 to 2025, reaching a market value of $1 trillion by 2025 (Grand View Research). The rising importance placed on sustainability and environmental issues has led consumers to favor eco-friendly products, creating a substantial opportunity for companies offering groundbreaking solutions.

B. Market Trends:
1. Increasing preference for biodegradable and compostable packaging materials due to their minimal ecological footprint.
2. Enhanced adoption of intelligent packaging technologies, such as RFID tags, QR codes, and sensors, to boost product traceability and freshness.
3. Stronger emphasis on decreasing plastic waste and implementing circular economy
