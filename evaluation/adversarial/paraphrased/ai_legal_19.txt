1. The Processor must adhere to all applicable privacy and data protection laws, regulations, and industry standards (collectively "Data Privacy Standards").

IV. Instructions from the Controller

1. The Processor can only process Personal Data based on documented instructions from the Controller, unless mandated by a Union or Member State law applicable to the Processor; in such cases, the Processor will inform the Controller before processing (unless the law prohibits disclosure for important reasons of public interest).
2. All Controller's instructions for handling Personal Data must be compliant with Data Privacy Standards.
