Title: Maximizing Solar Panel Productivity: A Comparative Examination of Monocrystalline and Polycrystalline Silicon Photovoltaic Systems

Introduction

In the ever-evolving global energy sector, renewable energy sources have taken center stage as the world confronts climate change and the depletion of fossil fuel resources. Solar photovoltaic (PV) technology has emerged as a potential answer for sustainable electricity generation, with silicon-based PV systems dominating the market due to their cost-effectiveness and high productivity. This study seeks to compare the performance of monocrystalline (mono-Si) and polycrystalline silicon (poly-Si) PV systems under standard test conditions, with a focus on identifying potential avenues for enhancing their productivity.

Research Approach

A comparative analysis was performed using commercially available mono-Si and poly-Si modules from prominent manufacturers. The modules were subjected to standardized laboratory testing in accordance with International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) guidelines, encompassing irradiance levels, temperature coefficients, and voltage-current characteristics. Furthermore, the influence of module design factors such as cell size, anti-reflection coatings, and encapsulation materials was explored.

Primary Results

The findings indicated that mono-Si systems exhibited a higher productivity (18.2%) compared to poly-Si systems (16.7%), primarily due to their smaller grain size, superior crystalline quality, and optimized cell design. Nevertheless, poly-Si systems presented lower manufacturing costs resulting from simpler production processes and less demanding raw material specifications. Moreover, the study pinpointed several strategies for improving the productivity of both types of systems, such as incorporating advanced cell structures (for instance, heterojunction with intrinsic thin layer, HIT), utilizing high-transmission encapsulation materials, and integrating anti-reflective coatings.

Consequences

This research offers significant insights into the performance characteristics and potential for productivity enhancement of mono-Si and poly-Si PV systems. The results underscore the need to strike a balance between cost and productivity
