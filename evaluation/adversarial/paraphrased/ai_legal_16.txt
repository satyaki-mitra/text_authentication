Title: Data Privacy Charter - Section 9: Individual's Rights Over Data

9.1 Individuals' Rights Over Data
  9.1.1 The Data Manager recognizes that individuals have certain privileges under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other pertinent privacy laws, which must be honored consistently. These privileges encompass:

   9.1.1.1 Right to Know (Article 15 GDPR): Individuals have the entitlement to request confirmation from the Data Manager whether their personal data is being processed. If affirmative, they are entitled to access this information along with details regarding processing.

   9.1.1.2 Right to Amend (Article 16 GDPR): Individuals can demand, without delay, the correction of any inaccurate personal data from the Data Manager. Given the purpose of processing, individuals may also request completion of incomplete data by supplying an additional statement.

   9.1.1.3 Right to Delete (Article 17 GDPR): Individuals have the right to request the Data Manager to erase their personal data without unnecessary delay under specified conditions. The Data Manager has an obligation to perform this action promptly.

   9.1.1.4 Right to Limit Processing (Article 18 GDPR): Individuals can request restriction of processing when certain conditions are met.

   9.1.1.5 Right to Object (Article 21 GDPR): Individuals may object, on legitimate grounds pertaining to their specific situation, to the processing of personal data based on points (e) or (f) of Article 6(1) GDPR at any time.

   9.1.1.6 Right to Data Migration (Article 20 GDPR): Individuals are entitled to receive their personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format from the controller they provided it to. They can also transmit these data to another controller without hindrance from the initial controller.

   9.1.2 Exercise of Individuals' Rights
      9.1.2.1 The Data Manager will establish a procedure for individuals to exercise their rights as detailed in Section 9.1.1. This process should be user-friendly, cost-free, and easily accessible.
