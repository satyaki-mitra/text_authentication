**Agreement Regarding Data Handling ("ADH")**

**Establishment**

This Agreement on Data Management ("Contract") is hereby established between [Service Provider Name] ("Data Manager") and [Company Name] ("Data Owner"), each a "Party" and together the "Parties." This Contract serves as an addition to the existing agreements between the Parties (the "Primary Agreement") and pertains to the handling of Personal Information by the Data Manager on behalf of the Data Owner, in accordance with the Primary Agreement.

**Objective**

The objective of this ADH is to guarantee that both Parties meet their respective obligations as specified under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and Council of 27 April 2016 on data protection, namely Regulation (GDPR).

**Terminology**

For the sake of clarity in this ADH, terms not defined herein shall bear the meanings attributed to them under GDPR or the Primary Agreement.

**Data Processing**

The Data Manager will process Personal Information solely for the purpose(s) and extent required to fulfill obligations as outlined within the Primary Agreement. The Data Manager will execute these tasks in accordance with the instructions provided by the Data Owner, unless mandated otherwise by applicable laws governing the Data Manager's operations.

**Subject Rights**

In light of the nature of the processing, the Data Manager will implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to help the Data Owner uphold its obligations in responding to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under GDPR.

**Security Protocols**

The Data Manager will establish and maintain suitable technical and organizational measures to ensure a security level commensurate with the associated risks, taking into account factors such as technological advancements, cost implications, the nature, scope, context, and purposes of processing, as well as the potential impacts on individuals' rights and freedoms.

**Subcontracting**

The Data Manager will not engage another processor without prior written authorization from the Data Owner, whether specific or general. In cases of general authorization, the Data Manager will inform the Data Owner about any planned changes concerning the addition or substitution of other processors, allowing the Data Owner to object to such modifications if necessary.

**Data Protection Assessment and Consultation**

The Data Manager will provide necessary assistance to the Data Owner in conducting data protection assessments and consultations as required under GDPR.
