Title: Data Handling Agreement (DHA) Provisions

1. Preamble

This Data Handling Agreement ("DHA") is formed between [Recipient's Name] ("Data Processor") and [Company Name] ("Data Controller"), each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties." The DHA serves as an extension of the [Agreement/Contract Name] (the "Agreement") between the Parties.

2. Aim

The primary objective of this DHA is to outline both parties' agreement on handling Personal Information in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council, enacted on April 27, 2016, concerning the protection of natural persons regarding personal data processing and the free movement of such data, replacing Directive 95/46/EC (General Data Protection Regulation) ("GDPR").

3. Terminology

For the purpose of this DHA:

3.1. "Personal Information" refers to any details pertaining to an identifiable natural person; such a person is one who can be identified, directly or indirectly, by means of a name, identification number, location data, online identifier, or specific characteristics related to physical, genetic, mental, economic, cultural, or social identity.

3.2. "Processing" encompasses any operation or series of operations performed on Personal Information, including but not limited to collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation or alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, dissemination, making available, alignment or combination, restriction, erasure, or destruction – regardless of whether automated means are employed.

4. Data Processing

The Data Processor shall process Personal Information solely for the purposes detailed in this DHA and as per the instructions provided by the Data Controller, as stipulated in the Agreement and any other written directives conveyed by the Data Controller at various times. The Data Processor will immediately inform the Data Controller if it believes that such an instruction contravenes applicable EU data protection law.

5. Security Measures

The Data Processor shall put into effect suitable technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security proportionate to the risks, taking into account factors such as the current state-of-the-art, costs of implementation, scope, context, purposes of processing, and associated risks for the rights and freedoms of natural persons.
