Title: Examining the Influence of Diet Variety on Cognitive Performance in Seniors: A Cross-Sectional Survey

Introduction:
The worldwide elderly population is experiencing an unparalleled expansion, accompanied by a surge in age-linked cognitive deterioration and related conditions like dementia. As preventive measures are increasingly sought, diet has emerged as a potential adjustable factor that could impact cognitive wellbeing. This study investigates the correlation between diet variety and cognitive performance among senior citizens.

Research Approach:
Utilizing a cross-sectional design, we gathered data from 1000 elderly individuals living independently in urban and rural regions of an average-sized American city. Dietary consumption was analyzed through a validated food frequency questionnaire, while cognitive performance was assessed using the Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA). Linear regression analyses were performed to explore the relationship between diet variety and MoCA scores, considering variables such as age, gender, education level, physical activity, and medical conditions.

Primary Conclusions:
The results revealed a substantial positive correlation between diet variety and cognitive performance (β = 0.25, p < 0.001). Higher diet variety scores correlated with superior execution in domains related to executive functions, memory, attention, and language on the MoCA test. Furthermore, our findings demonstrated that seniors consuming a diverse diet had lower probabilities of exhibiting cognitive deficits (OR = 0.68, p < 0.01).

Implications:
These results lend support to the notion that diet variety plays a role in preserving cognitive health among senior citizens. The findings emphasize the importance of advocating for diverse eating habits as a potential strategy for delaying age-related cognitive decline and dementia. Future research should focus on long-term studies to further substantiate the causal link between diet, diet variety, and cognitive performance. Additionally, initiatives aimed at boosting diet variety among seniors are merited, with the aim of enhancing cognitive health and quality of life in this vulnerable demographic.
