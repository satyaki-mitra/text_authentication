**Creating a Beautiful DIY Terrarium: A Step-by-Step Guide**

Terrariums are mini indoor gardens that require minimal maintenance and can thrive in any environment. With this guide, you'll learn how to create a stunning DIY terrarium using simple materials.

**Materials:**

* A glass container (e.g., jar, vase, or aquarium) with a wide mouth
* Small plants of your choice (e.g., succulents, mosses, ferns)
* Soil specifically designed for terrariums
* Pebbles or small rocks
* Activated charcoal (optional)
* Decorative stones or shells (optional)

**Step 1: Prepare the Container**

1. Clean the glass container thoroughly with soap and water.
2. Dry the container completely with a soft cloth.

**Step 2: Add a Layer of Small Rocks and Pebbles**

1. Fill the bottom third of the container with small rocks or pebbles to create drainage.
2. This layer should be about 1-2 inches thick.

**Step 3: Add Soil**

1. Fill the container with soil, leaving about an inch at the top for decoration.
2. Use a spoon or trowel to gently compact the soil.

**Step 4: Plant Your Selection**

1. Choose your plants and gently remove them from their pots.
2. Place the plants in the soil, arranging them as desired.
3. Fill any gaps with small amounts of soil or pebbles.

**Step 5: Add Activated Charcoal (Optional)**

1. If you're using activated charcoal, sprinkle a thin layer on top of the soil to help control moisture.
2. This is especially important if you're using plants that don't like wet conditions.

**Step 6: Add Decorative Stones or Shells (Optional)**

1. Use decorative stones or shells to create a visually appealing border around the container.
2. Arrange them as desired, making sure not to block any air vents.

**Step 7: Water and Seal**

1. Gently water your terrarium using a watering can or cup.
2. Place the lid on the container, but not too tightly – you want to allow for some air circulation.
3. Place your terrarium in a spot with bright, indirect light.

**Tips and Maintenance:**

* Avoid overwatering, as this can lead to mold and root rot.
* Mist the terrarium occasionally to maintain humidity.
* Avoid direct sunlight, which can cause temperature fluctuations.
* Prune plants regularly to maintain shape and encourage healthy growth.

By following these simple steps, you'll be able to create a stunning DIY terrarium that will thrive for months to come. Happy planting!
