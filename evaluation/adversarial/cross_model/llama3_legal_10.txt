Here's a sample formal legal clause for data privacy compliance:

**DATA PRIVACY COMPLIANCE**

1. **Definitions**: For the purposes of this clause, "Personal Data" means any information that identifies or could be used to identify an individual, including but not limited to name, address, phone number, email address, and other contact information.

2. **Compliance with Applicable Laws**: The [Party] (the "Data Controller") agrees to comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding the processing of Personal Data, including but not limited to:

a) The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) 2016/679; and

b) All relevant national laws, regulations, and guidelines that govern the processing of Personal Data.

3. **Data Processing**: The Data Controller shall process Personal Data only in accordance with this clause and for the purposes specified below:

a) [State specific purpose(s) for which the data will be processed, e.g. "to provide services", "to market products", etc.].

b) The Data Controller shall not use or disclose Personal Data except as necessary to fulfill its obligations under this clause or as permitted by applicable law.

4. **Data Subject Rights**: The Data Controller agrees to provide all individuals whose Personal Data it processes (the "Data Subjects") with the following rights:

a) The right of access to their Personal Data;

b) The right to rectify inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data;

c) The right to erasure of Personal Data (also known as the "right to be forgotten");

d) The right to restriction on processing Personal Data;

e) The right to object to the processing of Personal Data; and

f) The right to data portability.
