Here is a sample formal legal clause on data privacy compliance:

**DATA PRIVACY COMPLIANCE**

**1. Purpose and Scope**

This Data Privacy Compliance Clause (the "Clause") sets forth the obligations of [Company Name] ("we" or "us") to protect the personal data of individuals who interact with us, including customers, clients, employees, and other stakeholders (collectively, "Individuals"). This Clause applies to all personal data processed by us in connection with our business operations.

**2. Compliance with Applicable Laws**

We shall comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards relating to the processing of personal data, including but not limited to:

* The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) 2016/679;
* The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) of 2018;
* Other national or international data privacy laws and regulations as may be applicable.

**3. Data Processing**

We shall process personal data in accordance with the following principles:

* Lawfulness: We shall only process personal data that is necessary for a legitimate purpose and that has been obtained fairly and lawfully.
* Purpose Limitation: We shall specify the purposes for which personal data is processed and limit processing to those purposes.
* Data Minimization: We shall collect and process only the personal data that is necessary to achieve the specified purposes.
* Accuracy: We shall take reasonable steps to ensure that personal data is accurate, complete, and up-to-date.
* Storage Limitation: We shall retain personal data for no longer than necessary to achieve the specified purposes.

**4. Data Subject Rights**

We recognize the rights of Individuals to:

* Access their personal data;
* Rectify inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
* Erase their personal data under certain circumstances; and
* Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling.
