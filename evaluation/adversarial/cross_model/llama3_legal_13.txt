**ARTICLE III: REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES**

1. **Definition of Representations and Warranties**: As used in this Article, the term "Representations and Warranties" means all statements, assurances, and promises made by either party to this Agreement.

2. **Warranty of Authority**: Each party represents and warrants that it has the full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and perform its obligations hereunder.

3. **Accuracy of Information**: Each party represents and warrants that:
	(a) The information provided in its executed signature page is true, accurate, and complete; and
	(b) It will promptly notify the other party in writing of any change in the circumstances described on such signature page.

4. **No Conflicting Obligations**: Each party represents and warrants that it does not have any obligation or commitment to a third party that would prevent it from fulfilling its obligations under this Agreement.

5. **Compliance with Laws**: Each party represents and warrants that:
	(a) It will comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and ordinances in performing its obligations under this Agreement; and
	(b) Its performance of its obligations hereunder does not violate any law or regulation.

6. **No Conflict of Interest**: Each party represents and warrants that it has no conflict of interest that would prevent it from acting in good faith to fulfill its obligations under this Agreement.

7. **Survival of Representations and Warranties**: The representations and warranties made by each party under this Article III shall survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement and remain in full force and effect until all obligations under this Agreement have been fulfilled.

**EFFECTIVE DATE**

This Article III, together with the remainder of this Agreement, is effective as of the date first written above.
