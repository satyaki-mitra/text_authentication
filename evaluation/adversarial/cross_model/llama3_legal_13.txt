Here is a draft of a formal legal clause regarding data privacy compliance:

**DATA PRIVACY COMPLIANCE**

[Insert Company Name] ("Company") values the privacy and security of personal data and is committed to complying with all applicable laws and regulations regarding data privacy, including but not limited to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) in the United States, and other relevant data protection laws.

**COMPLIANCE WITH DATA PRIVACY LAWS**

The Company agrees to comply with all applicable data privacy laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

* Collecting, processing, storing, and transmitting personal data only as necessary for legitimate business purposes;
* Providing clear and conspicuous notice to individuals about the purposes for which their personal data is collected, processed, stored, and transmitted;
* Obtaining valid consent from individuals before collecting, processing, storing, or transmitting their personal data;
* Maintaining accurate, complete, and up-to-date records of all personal data processing activities;
* Implementing reasonable security measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction;
* Allowing individuals to exercise their rights under applicable data privacy laws, including but not limited to the right to access, correct, delete, and object to the processing of their personal data;

**AUDITS AND REPORTING**

The Company agrees to conduct regular audits and assessments to ensure compliance with all applicable data privacy laws and regulations. The Company will maintain records of such audits and assessments, which will be made available upon request.

**NOTICE OF DATA BREACHES**

In the event of a data breach or unauthorized access to personal data, the Company will notify affected individuals without undue delay and in accordance with applicable law. The Company will also cooperate fully with any subsequent investigations and regulatory actions.
