Here is a draft of a formal legal clause about data privacy compliance:

**Data Privacy Compliance**

1. **Definitions**: For purposes of this Section, "Personal Data" means any information that (a) identifies an individual, or (b) is directly or indirectly related to an individual and could be used to identify such individual.

2. **Compliance with Applicable Laws**: [Name of Company] will comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards regarding the collection, processing, storage, transmission, use, and disposal of Personal Data ("Data Privacy Laws").

3. **Data Protection Principles**: [Name of Company] will implement and maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect Personal Data from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction in accordance with the following principles:

(a) **Collection Limitation**: [Name of Company] will only collect Personal Data that is necessary for a legitimate purpose and will inform individuals of the purposes for which their Personal Data will be used.

(b) **Data Quality**: [Name of Company] will ensure that all Personal Data collected, processed, or stored by it is accurate, complete, and up-to-date.

(c) **Purpose Limitation**: [Name of Company] will only use Personal Data in a manner consistent with the purposes for which it was collected and will not use such data for any purpose that is incompatible with those purposes.

(d) **Security**: [Name of Company] will take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction of Personal Data.

(e) **Openness and Transparency**: [Name of Company] will maintain accurate records of all Personal Data collection, processing, and storage activities, including the categories of individuals whose Personal Data is collected, processed, or stored, and will provide such information to individuals upon request.
