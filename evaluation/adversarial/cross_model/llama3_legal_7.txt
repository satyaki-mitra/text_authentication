**ARTICLE III: CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY**

Section 1. Confidential Information.

The Parties agree that all information exchanged or disclosed during the course of this Agreement shall be considered confidential and proprietary to one or both of the Parties, as applicable. "Confidential Information" means any and all information related to the Parties' businesses, including but not limited to, trade secrets, know-how, inventions, patents, trademarks, copyrights, and other intellectual property rights.

Section 2. Confidentiality Obligations.

Each Party agrees that it shall:

(a) use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of fulfilling its obligations under this Agreement;

(b) keep all Confidential Information confidential and not disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent from the disclosing Party; and

(c) take all reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized disclosure or use of the Confidential Information.

Section 3. Exclusions.

The confidentiality obligations set forth in Section 2 shall not apply to:

(a) information that is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party;

(b) information already known to the receiving Party at the time of disclosure; or

(c) information developed independently by the receiving Party without reference to the Confidential Information.

Section 4. Ownership and License.

The Parties acknowledge that all intellectual property rights, including but not limited to patents, trademarks, copyrights, and trade secrets, are and shall remain the exclusive property of each Party, as applicable. Each Party grants to the other a non-exclusive license to use, reproduce, distribute, and display the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of fulfilling its obligations under this Agreement.

Section 5. Returns.

Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, each Party agrees to return all Confidential Information in its possession or control to the disclosing Party, or destroy such information and certify that it has been destroyed.

**By executing this Agreement, the Parties acknowledge that they have read, understand, and agree to be bound by the terms and conditions set forth in Article III.**
