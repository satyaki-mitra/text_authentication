Here is a draft of a formal legal clause about data privacy compliance:

**DATA PRIVACY COMPLIANCE**

1. **Purpose**: The parties acknowledge that in the course of their respective businesses, they may collect, process, and store personal data (as defined below) from or about individuals. The purpose of this clause is to ensure that each party complies with applicable data privacy laws and regulations, including but not limited to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended from time to time.

2. **Definitions**:

* "Personal data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual, including but not limited to name, email address, phone number, IP address, biometric data, and other identifying characteristics.
* "Data controller" refers to the party responsible for determining the purposes and means of processing personal data.

3. **Compliance with Data Privacy Laws**: Each party agrees to comply with all applicable data privacy laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

* The GDPR and any implementing legislation;
* The CCPA and any implementing legislation;
* Any other federal, state, or local data privacy laws and regulations in effect from time to time;

4. **Data Processing**: Each party shall only process personal data (a) as necessary for the purposes of this Agreement; (b) in accordance with applicable law; and (c) in a manner that is consistent with the parties' respective privacy policies.

5. **Notice and Transparency**: Each party shall provide clear and conspicuous notice to individuals about its data processing practices, including but not limited to:

* The types of personal data collected;
* The purposes for which such data is collected;
* The categories of third parties with whom such data may be shared;
* The rights of individuals to access, correct, or delete their personal data;

6. **Individual Rights**: Each party shall respect the rights of individuals to:

* Access their personal data upon request
