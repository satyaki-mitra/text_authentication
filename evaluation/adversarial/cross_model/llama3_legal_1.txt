Here's a sample formal legal clause on data privacy compliance:

**Data Privacy Compliance**

1. **Definitions**: For the purposes of this Agreement, "Personal Data" means any information that can be used to identify an individual, including but not limited to name, address, phone number, email address, and other demographic or biometric information.

2. **Compliance with Applicable Laws**: The parties agree to comply with all applicable laws and regulations related to the processing of Personal Data, including but not limited to:

a) The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) 2016/679 of the European Union;

b) The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), if applicable;

c) Any other applicable data protection laws or regulations.

3. **Data Processing**: Each party agrees that it will only process Personal Data:

a) As necessary to fulfill its obligations under this Agreement;

b) In accordance with the written instructions of the other party, as applicable; and

c) In compliance with all applicable laws and regulations related to data protection.

4. **Security Measures**: The parties agree to implement and maintain reasonable security measures to protect Personal Data from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction.

5. **Data Breach Response**: In the event of a suspected data breach involving Personal Data, each party agrees to:

a) Promptly notify the other party in writing of the suspected breach;

b) Cooperate with the other party to investigate and respond to the breach; and

c) Take all reasonable steps to minimize the harm caused by the breach.

6. **Data Subject Rights**: Each party agrees to respect the rights of data subjects, including but not limited to:

a) The right to access their Personal Data;

b) The right to rectify inaccuracies in their Personal Data;

c) The right to erase their Personal Data; and

d) The right to restrict or object to the processing of their Personal Data.
