**SECTION 4: CONFIDENTIALITY AND PROPRIETARY INFORMATION**

The Parties agree that certain information, including but not limited to trade secrets, confidential business information, and proprietary data, shall be kept confidential and protected by this Agreement.

**DEFINITIONS**

For the purposes of this Section, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

* "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed or made available to a Party (the "Disclosing Party") by the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether written or oral, that is not generally known in the industry and that the Disclosing Party considers to be confidential or proprietary.
* "Proprietary Information" means any Confidential Information that is embodied in or represented by intellectual property rights, including but not limited to patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, and any applications therefor.

**CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS**

The Receiving Party agrees:

1. To maintain the confidentiality of all Confidential Information disclosed by the Disclosing Party;
2. Not to use or disclose any Confidential Information for its own benefit or the benefit of others, except as permitted under this Agreement or with the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party;
3. To take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information, including but not limited to:
	* Limiting access to Confidential Information to employees and agents having a need to know;
	* Using passwords, encryption, and other security measures to protect Confidential Information; and
	* Returning or destroying all Confidential Information upon the expiration or termination of this Agreement.
4. To notify the Disclosing Party promptly in writing if the Receiving Party becomes aware of any unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information.

**PROPRIETARY INFORMATION**

The Receiving Party acknowledges that the Proprietary Information is and shall remain the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party, and agrees not to:

1. Use or disclose any Proprietary Information for its own benefit or the benefit of others;
2. File, prosecute, or abandon any application for intellectual property rights related to the Proprietary Information;
3. Allow any third party to use or disclose any Proprietary Information.

**TERM AND TERMINATION**

The confidentiality obligations under this Section shall survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years following the date of termination, after which all Confidential Information and Proprietary Information shall be returned or destroyed.
