(a) The other Party fails to comply with any material provision of this Agreement, and such failure is not cured within [insert number] days after receipt of written notice from the non-breaching Party;

(a) All rights and obligations of the Parties shall cease, except as provided in subsections (b) and (c);

(b) Each Party shall promptly return all Confidential Information and any other materials received from the other Party under this Agreement;

(c) The following provisions of this Agreement shall survive termination: [insert specific provisions].

**3.4 Survival.** Sections 2.5, 3.1, and 4.6 (as applicable) shall survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.
