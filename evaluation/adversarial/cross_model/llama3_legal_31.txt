**ARTICLE III: OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES**

1. **DEFINITIONS**: As used in this Article, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

* "Obligations" refers to all duties, responsibilities, and liabilities owed by the Parties to each other under this Agreement.
* "Responsible Party" means the Party that is responsible for fulfilling its Obligations.

2. **GENERAL OBLIGATIONS**: Each Party agrees to fulfill its respective Obligations in a timely and professional manner. In the event of any failure to meet such Obligations, the affected Party may take all necessary steps to protect its interests, including seeking injunctive relief or terminating this Agreement as provided in Article VII.

3. **SPECIFIC OBLIGATIONS**:

* **Party A**: Party A agrees to deliver all necessary documents and materials to Party B within five (5) business days of the Effective Date. Party A shall ensure that such documents are accurate, complete, and compliant with applicable laws.
* **Party B**: Party B agrees to provide a written response to Party A's inquiries and requests for information within two (2) business days.

4. **DEFAULT AND REMEDIES**:

* If either Party fails to fulfill its Obligations in a timely manner, the other Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon notice.
* In addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, the Parties agree that the non-breaching Party shall be entitled to seek compensatory damages and/or specific performance of the Obligations.

5. **GOVERNING LAW**: This Article shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [State/Country]. Any disputes arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration in [Location], as provided in Article VIII.
