Here is a draft of a formal legal clause regarding data privacy compliance:

**DATA PRIVACY COMPLIANCE**

1. **Definition**: For the purposes of this Agreement, "Personal Data" means any information that identifies or can be used to identify an individual, including but not limited to name, address, phone number, email address, and other personal information.

2. **Data Protection Principles**: [Party Name] agrees to process Personal Data in accordance with the following principles:

a) **Lawfulness**: All processing of Personal Data shall be lawful, fair, and transparent;

b) **Purpose Limitation**: Personal Data shall only be processed for the purpose(s) specified by this Agreement or as otherwise permitted by applicable law;

c) **Data Minimization**: Only Personal Data necessary to achieve the purpose(s) shall be collected and processed;

d) **Accuracy**: All Personal Data shall be accurate, complete, and up-to-date;

e) **Storage Limitation**: Personal Data shall only be stored for a period of time that is necessary for the purpose(s) specified by this Agreement or as otherwise permitted by applicable law;

f) **Integrity and Confidentiality**: All Personal Data shall be processed in a manner that ensures its integrity and confidentiality, including but not limited to using appropriate technical and organizational measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of Personal Data.

3. **Data Controller and Processor Responsibilities**:

a) **Controller**: [Party Name] is the "Data Controller" responsible for ensuring compliance with applicable data protection laws and regulations;

b) **Processor**: [Other Party/Third Party] (the "Data Processor") shall process Personal Data only as instructed by [Party Name], in accordance with this Agreement, and in compliance with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

4. **Security Measures**:

a) **Technical Security Measures**: [Party Name] and the Data Processor shall implement appropriate technical security measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction
