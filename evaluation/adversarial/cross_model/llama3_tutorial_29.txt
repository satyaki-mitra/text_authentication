**Guided Practice: Creating a Simple Spreadsheet**

**Objective:** To create a basic spreadsheet using Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel, demonstrating essential skills in data organization and visualization.

**Materials:**

* Computer with internet access
* Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel software installed
* A simple dataset (e.g., student grades)

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Open the Spreadsheet Software**
	* Launch Google Sheets (gsuite.google.com) or Microsoft Excel (excel.office.com) on your computer.
2. **Create a New Document**
	* In Google Sheets, click on the "New" button and select "Google Sheets." In Microsoft Excel, click on the "File" menu and choose "New."
3. **Set Up Your Spreadsheet**
	* Give your spreadsheet a title (e.g., "Student Grades") and set the dimensions to 5 rows x 2 columns.
4. **Enter Your Data**
	* Copy and paste your simple dataset into the spreadsheet, using one row for each student's information (e.g., name, grade, subject).
	* Use the first column for student names and the second column for grades.

**Tips:**

* Keep your data organized by using clear labels and formatting.
* Use formulas to perform basic calculations, such as averaging or summing scores.

5. **Format Your Data**
	* Use the "Format" tab (Google Sheets) or "Home" tab (Microsoft Excel) to apply styles to your data.
	* Choose a font size and color scheme that is easy to read.
6. **Create Charts and Graphs**
	* Select the range of cells containing the grades data.
	* Click on the "Insert" menu (Google Sheets) or "Chart Tools" tab (Microsoft Excel).
	* Choose a simple chart type, such as a bar graph or pie chart.

**Additional Tips:**

* Use hover-over text to provide additional information about each student's grade.
* Experiment with different chart types and colors to visualize your data effectively.

**Conclusion:**
Congratulations! You have successfully created a basic spreadsheet using Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel. Practice these essential skills to improve your ability to organize, analyze, and present data in various formats.
