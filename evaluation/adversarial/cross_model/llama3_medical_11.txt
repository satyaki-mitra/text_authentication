**Title:** Rare Case of Congenital Pulmonary Artery Hypoplasia Presenting as Recurrent Pneumonia in an Adult

**Abstract:**

We report a rare and challenging case of congenital pulmonary artery hypoplasia (CPAH) presenting as recurrent pneumonia in a 35-year-old adult. The patient initially presented with a three-month history of recurrent left upper lobe pneumonia, unresponsive to antibiotic therapy. Physical examination revealed tachypnea, wheezing, and decreased breath sounds on the left side. Chest radiography showed persistent opacification of the left upper lobe.

Further evaluation included computed tomography (CT) scan of the chest, which revealed a severely hypoplastic right pulmonary artery and an anomalous origin of the left pulmonary artery from the aorta. Echocardiogram showed normal left ventricular function but mild tricuspid regurgitation. Pulmonary function tests (PFTs) demonstrated moderate obstructive lung disease.

The patient underwent surgical correction, including plication of the hypoplastic right pulmonary artery and creation of a functional connection between the left pulmonary artery and the right pulmonary artery. Postoperatively, the patient experienced significant improvement in symptoms, with resolution of recurrent pneumonia episodes.

CPAH is a rare congenital anomaly affecting 1 in 10,000 to 1 in 50,000 individuals. In adults, CPAH typically presents as exercise intolerance, cyanosis, or dyspnea. However, our case highlights the unusual presentation of recurrent pneumonia due to impaired pulmonary blood flow and ventilation-perfusion mismatch.

In conclusion, this case illustrates the importance of considering rare congenital anomalies in adult patients with unexplained respiratory symptoms. A high index of suspicion and a multidisciplinary approach are essential for accurate diagnosis and effective management of these complex cases.

**Keywords:** Congenital Pulmonary Artery Hypoplasia; Recurrent Pneumonia; Adult Presentation; Pulmonary Function Tests; Echocardiogram; Computed Tomography Scan.
