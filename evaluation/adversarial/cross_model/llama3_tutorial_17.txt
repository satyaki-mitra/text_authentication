**Cooking Perfect Scrambled Eggs: A Step-by-Step Guide**

Are you ready to learn how to cook fluffy and delicious scrambled eggs? With these simple steps, you'll be a pro in no time!

**Materials Needed:**

* 2 eggs per serving
* Salt and pepper to taste
* Butter or oil for greasing the pan

**Step 1: Crack the Eggs (Time: 30 seconds)**

1. Gently crack each egg into a bowl or ramekin.
2. Make sure not to get any shells into the eggs.

**Step 2: Whisk the Eggs (Time: 1 minute)**

1. Use a fork or whisk to beat the eggs until they're well mixed and frothy.
2. Add a pinch of salt and pepper to taste.

**Step 3: Heat the Pan (Time: 2 minutes)**

1. Choose a non-stick pan over medium heat.
2. Add a small amount of butter or oil to grease the pan.
3. Let it melt and get hot, but not smoking hot!

**Step 4: Pour in the Eggs (Time: 30 seconds)**

1. Slowly pour the egg mixture into the heated pan.
2. Make sure to pour them evenly around the pan.

**Step 5: Cook the Eggs (Time: 3-4 minutes)**

1. Use a spatula to gently push the cooked eggs towards the center of the pan, allowing the uncooked egg to flow to the edges.
2. Repeat this process for about 3-4 minutes, or until the eggs are almost set.

**Step 6: Finish and Serve (Time: 30 seconds)**

1. Use your spatula to break up any large curds into smaller, fluffy pieces.
2. Remove the pan from heat and let the eggs sit for 10-15 seconds.
3. Use your spatula to scoop out the scrambled eggs onto a plate or toast.

**Tips and Variations:**

* For fluffier eggs, whisk them just before cooking!
* Add diced veggies (e.g., bell peppers, mushrooms) or shredded cheese to the eggs for extra flavor.
* Try using different seasonings like paprika or chili powder for added spice!

By following these simple steps, you'll be cooking perfect scrambled eggs in no time! Remember to always use fresh eggs and a non-stick pan to ensure the best results. Happy cooking!
