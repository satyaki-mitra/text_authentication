**SECTION 5: CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE**

1. **DEFINITIONS**: For the purposes of this Section, "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would consider confidential given the nature and circumstances of the disclosure.

2. **CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS**: Each party agrees:

a) To hold Confidential Information in confidence and not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the other party;

b) Not to use Confidential Information for any purpose other than as contemplated by this Agreement, including but not limited to developing a product or service that is similar to the other party's product or service;

c) To take all reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information, including limiting access to such information to authorized personnel and implementing appropriate technical and procedural safeguards.

3. **EXCEPTIONS**: The obligations under this Section shall not apply to:

a) Confidential Information that is or becomes generally available to the public through no fault of the disclosing party;

b) Confidential Information that was in the possession of the receiving party prior to the disclosure;

c) Confidential Information that is disclosed by the disclosing party with the prior written consent of the other party;

d) Confidential Information that is required to be disclosed by law or regulation, provided that the disclosing party provides prompt notice of such requirement and cooperates with the receiving party to limit any resulting damage.

4. **RETURN OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**: Upon request, each party agrees to promptly return all Confidential Information in its possession to the other party, including all copies, extracts, and derivative works.

5. **SURVIVAL**: The obligations under this Section shall survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years from such date, and the parties agree that these obligations are reasonable and necessary to protect the interests of the disclosing party.
