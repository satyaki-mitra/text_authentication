Here is a draft of a formal legal clause about data privacy compliance:

**Data Privacy Compliance**

[Insert Company Name] (the "Company") recognizes the importance of protecting personal and sensitive information, and is committed to complying with all applicable laws and regulations regarding data privacy and security.

To that end, the Company agrees to take the following steps to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and security of all Personal Data (as defined below):

1. **Applicability**: This clause shall apply to all Personal Data collected, stored, processed or transmitted by or on behalf of the Company, including but not limited to information related to employees, customers, users, visitors, and any other individuals whose data is processed in connection with the provision of products and services by the Company.
2. **Definition of Personal Data**: For the purposes of this clause, "Personal Data" means any information that can be used to identify an individual, including but not limited to name, address, email address, phone number, IP address, location data, cookies, or other online identifiers.
3. **Data Collection and Processing**: The Company shall only collect and process Personal Data in accordance with the following principles:
	* Lawful basis: The Company shall only collect and process Personal Data that is necessary for a legitimate business purpose, and shall obtain valid consent from the individual prior to collecting and processing sensitive or special categories of data.
	* Transparency: The Company shall clearly and conspicuously communicate its data collection and processing practices to individuals prior to collecting their Personal Data.
	* Purpose limitation: The Company shall only collect and process Personal Data for a specific, legitimate purpose, and shall not use the data for any other purpose without obtaining valid consent from the individual.
	* Minimization: The Company shall minimize the amount of Personal Data collected and processed to only that which is necessary for its intended purpose.
	* Accuracy: The Company shall take reasonable steps to ensure the accuracy and completeness of all Personal Data it collects and processes.
