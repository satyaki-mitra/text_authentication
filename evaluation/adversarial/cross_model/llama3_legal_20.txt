Here is a sample formal legal clause about data privacy compliance:

**DATA PRIVACY COMPLIANCE**

1. **Definitions**: For the purposes of this Agreement, the following definitions shall apply:
   - "Personal Data" means any information that identifies or could be used to identify an individual, including but not limited to name, address, email address, phone number, and other identifying information.
   - "Data Protection Laws" means all applicable laws and regulations related to data privacy and protection, including but not limited to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and any other similar laws in jurisdictions where [Company Name] operates.

2. **Compliance with Data Protection Laws**: [Company Name] shall comply with all applicable Data Protection Laws when processing Personal Data, including but not limited to:
   - Collecting, storing, using, disclosing, and transferring Personal Data only in accordance with the purposes for which it was collected;
   - Providing adequate notice to individuals whose Personal Data is being processed, including a description of the types of data being collected and the purposes for which it will be used;
   - Obtaining valid consent from individuals before processing their Personal Data, except where permitted by applicable law;
   - Implementing reasonable security measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, or destruction;
   - Providing a mechanism for individuals to exercise their rights under applicable Data Protection Laws, including the right to access, correct, delete, and/or object to the processing of their Personal Data;

3. **Responsibilities**: [Company Name] shall be responsible for ensuring compliance with all applicable Data Protection Laws when processing Personal Data on its own behalf or on behalf of a third party.
