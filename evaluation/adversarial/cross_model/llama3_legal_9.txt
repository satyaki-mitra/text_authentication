**ARTICLE IV: CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY**

**SECTION 1: CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**

1.1 Definitions. For purposes of this Article, "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether orally or in writing, that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would consider confidential under the circumstances.

1.2 Confidential Treatment. The Receiving Party shall treat all Confidential Information with the same degree of care and confidentiality that it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

1.3 Permitted Disclosures. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only:

(a) to its employees, contractors, or agents who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations; or

(b) as required by law, regulation, or court order, provided that the Receiving Party gives prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party of such disclosure.

**SECTION 2: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS**

2.1 Ownership. Each Party shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to its Intellectual Property (as defined below), including any patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, and other intellectual property rights, whether developed before or after the Effective Date of this Agreement.

2.2 License Grant. Each Party grants the other Party a non-exclusive license to use, modify, reproduce, and distribute its Intellectual Property solely for the purposes of performing its obligations under this Agreement.

2.3 Representations and Warranties. Each Party represents and warrants that:

(a) it has the right to grant the licenses and permissions contemplated in this Article; and

(b) its Intellectual Property does not infringe or misappropriate any third-party intellectual property rights.

**SECTION 3: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY INFRINGEMENT**

3.1 Indemnification. The Party whose Intellectual Property is allegedly infringed (the "Claimant") shall notify the other Party in writing of such alleged infringement, and the Claimant and the other Party shall cooperate to resolve the issue promptly.

3.2 Defense and Settlement. If a claim or allegation of intellectual property infringement is made against either Party, the Claimant shall have the right to control the defense and settlement of such claim, at its own expense. The Claimant shall keep the other Party informed of all material developments in the case.

**SECTION 4: SURVIVAL**

This Article IV shall survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years from the Effective Date, during which time each Party shall maintain the confidentiality and protect the Intellectual Property as provided in this Article.
