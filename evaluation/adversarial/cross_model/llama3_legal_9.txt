Here is a draft of a formal legal clause about data privacy compliance:

**DATA PRIVACY COMPLIANCE**

1. **Compliance with Applicable Laws**: [Company Name] shall comply with all applicable laws and regulations relating to the protection of personal data, including but not limited to: [list specific laws and regulations, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), etc.].

2. **Data Processing**: [Company Name] shall only process personal data in a manner that is consistent with the purposes for which it was collected or processed, and shall ensure that any third-party processors or sub-processors are compliant with applicable laws and regulations regarding data processing.

3. **Data Subject Rights**: [Company Name] shall respect the rights of data subjects to access, correct, delete, and otherwise manage their personal data in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, including but not limited to: [list specific rights, such as right of access, correction, erasure, etc.].

4. **Security Measures**: [Company Name] shall implement and maintain reasonable security measures to protect against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of personal data, including but not limited to: [list specific security measures, such as encryption, firewalls, password protection, etc.].

5. **Data Breach Notification**: In the event of a breach of personal data, [Company Name] shall notify affected individuals and relevant regulatory authorities in accordance with applicable laws and regulations regarding data breach notification, including but not limited to: [list specific notification requirements, such as time frames for notification, content of notification, etc.].

6. **Data Retention**: [Company Name] shall retain personal data only for the period necessary to achieve the purposes for which it was collected or processed, and shall ensure that any retained data is properly anonymized or deleted in accordance with applicable laws and regulations regarding data retention.
