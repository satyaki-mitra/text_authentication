Here is a draft of a formal legal clause about data privacy compliance:

**DATA PRIVACY COMPLIANCE**

1. **Definitions**: As used in this Clause, "Personal Data" means any information that identifies or could be used to identify an individual, including but not limited to name, address, phone number, email address, IP address, and other personally identifiable information.

2. **Compliance with Applicable Laws**: [Party A] agrees to comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding the collection, use, disclosure, storage, and destruction of Personal Data ("Data Privacy Laws"), including but not limited to:

* The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679;
* The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California Civil Code Section 1798.100 et seq.;
* The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA);
* The Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA); and
* Any other Data Privacy Laws applicable to the processing of Personal Data.

3. **Data Collection**: [Party A] shall only collect Personal Data that is necessary for the purpose(s) stated in this Agreement, and such collection shall be done in accordance with the applicable Data Privacy Laws.

4. **Data Use**: [Party A] shall use Personal Data only for the purpose(s) stated in this Agreement and shall not use such data for any other purpose without the prior written consent of [Party B].

5. **Data Disclosure**: [Party A] shall not disclose Personal Data to any third party without the prior written consent of [Party B], except as permitted by applicable Data Privacy Laws or as required by law.
