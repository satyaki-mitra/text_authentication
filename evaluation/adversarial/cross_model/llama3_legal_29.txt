**Section 1: Confidentiality Obligations**

2.1 In the event that a Party is required by law, regulation, or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it shall promptly notify the other Party and provide reasonable assistance in maintaining the confidentiality of such information.

3.1 Any invention, discovery, development, or improvement conceived, made, or reduced to practice during the Term of this Agreement shall be considered a "Work" for purposes of this Agreement.

3.3 The Parties agree that any Work will be considered a joint invention, and each Party shall have the right to use and exploit such Work for its own benefit, provided that it does not conflict with the rights of the other Party under this Agreement.

4.1 Each Party retains ownership and control over all Confidential Information, intellectual property, and proprietary information exchanged or disclosed during the Term of this Agreement.
