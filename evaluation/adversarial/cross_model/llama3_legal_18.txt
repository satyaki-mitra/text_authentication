Here is a sample formal legal clause about data privacy compliance:

**Data Privacy Compliance**

1. **Definitions**: For purposes of this Clause, "Personal Data" means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual, including but not limited to name, address, phone number, email address, IP address, login credentials, and any other personal information.

2. **Compliance with Applicable Laws**: [Name of Company] (the "Company") shall comply with all applicable data privacy laws, regulations, and guidelines in the jurisdictions where it operates, including but not limited to the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), the California Consumer Privacy Act ("CCPA"), and any other relevant laws or regulations that may be enacted from time to time.

3. **Data Collection**: The Company shall only collect Personal Data when necessary for the performance of its obligations under this [Agreement/Contract], and such collection shall be done in a manner that is fair, transparent, and lawful.

4. **Data Processing**: The Company shall process all Personal Data in accordance with the applicable data privacy laws and regulations, and shall ensure that any third-party processors or sub-processors are also subject to appropriate data protection measures.

5. **Data Subject Rights**: The Company shall provide all individuals whose Personal Data it collects or processes (the "Data Subjects") with the following rights:

* The right of access to their Personal Data;
* The right to rectify inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data;
* The right to erasure ("right to be forgotten");
* The right to restriction of processing;
* The right to object to processing; and
* The right to data portability.

6. **Breach Notification**: In the event that there is a breach of Personal Data, the Company shall notify the affected Data Subjects promptly, and in any event within [time frame], and shall provide such notification in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
