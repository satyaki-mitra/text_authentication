 Get moving: Whether it's a quick yoga flow or a walk around the block, exercise releases endorphins that boost your mood

 Connect with nature: Spend some quality time outdoors. Go for a hike, have a picnic, or simply sit in a park and people-watch

 Treat yourself: You deserve it! Whether it's a relaxing bath, a good book, or a favorite meal, make time for activities that bring you joy
