**ARTICLE V: CONFIDENTIALITY AND PROPRIETARY RIGHTS**

1. **DEFINITIONS**. For the purposes of this Article, "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by either Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, whether orally or in writing, including but not limited to trade secrets, business strategies, customer lists, financial data, and intellectual property.

2. **CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS**. Each Party agrees to keep Confidential Information confidential and not to disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the other Party. The receiving Party shall use the same degree of care in protecting the Confidential Information as it uses for its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

3. **EXEMPTIONS**. The confidentiality obligations set forth above shall not apply to any Confidential Information that:

a) is or becomes generally available to the public through no fault of the receiving Party;

b) was lawfully in possession of the receiving Party prior to the disclosure by the disclosing Party;

c) is lawfully obtained from a third party who has the right to disclose such information; or

d) is required to be disclosed by applicable laws, regulations, or judicial process.

4. **PROPRIETARY RIGHTS**. The Parties agree that all intellectual property rights in and to any Confidential Information shall remain the property of the disclosing Party. The receiving Party agrees not to use or exploit the Confidential Information for its own benefit or to the detriment of the disclosing Party.

5. **RETURN OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**. Upon request by the disclosing Party, the receiving Party shall return all Confidential Information and destroy any copies thereof.

6. **INDEMNIFICATION**. The receiving Party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the disclosing Party against any claims, damages, or expenses arising from any breach of this Article V by the receiving Party.

7. **SURVIVAL**. The confidentiality obligations set forth in this Article shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years following such termination or expiration.

By executing this Agreement, each Party acknowledges that it has read, understands, and agrees to be bound by the terms and conditions of this Article V.
