**ARTICLE III: CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY**

This Article sets forth the parties' obligations regarding confidentiality, intellectual property, and proprietary information.

**3.1 Confidential Information.** For purposes of this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by one party to the other that is not generally known or available to the public, including but not limited to:

* Trade secrets;
* Proprietary processes;
* Technical data;
* Financial information;
* Business strategies; and
* Non-public marketing and sales plans.

**3.2 Confidentiality Obligations.** Each party agrees to:

* Keep all Confidential Information confidential and not disclose it to any third party;
* Use the same level of care in protecting Confidential Information as it would with its own confidential information;
* Limit access to Confidential Information only to those employees, agents, or contractors who have a legitimate need to know; and
* Not use or disclose Confidential Information for any purpose other than the purposes contemplated by this Agreement.

**3.3 Permitted Disclosures.** Each party may disclose Confidential Information:

* To its employees, agents, or contractors with a legitimate need to know;
* As required by law or court order; or
* In connection with any legal proceedings.

**3.4 Ownership and Intellectual Property.**

* The parties acknowledge that all intellectual property rights in and to their respective products, services, software, and other proprietary materials (the "IP") are and will remain the exclusive property of the party creating such IP.
* Neither party grants the other party any interest or right in or to its IP, except as specifically provided in this Agreement.

**3.5 Indemnification.**

* Each party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the other party from any claims, damages, or losses arising from a breach of this Article III.
* The indemnifying party shall notify the indemnified party promptly upon learning of any claim or potential claim that may be subject to indemnification.

**3.6 Survival.** The obligations set forth in this Article III will survive for a period of [X] years after the expiration or termination of this Agreement, regardless of whether such obligation is labeled as "surviving" or not.

By executing this Agreement, each party acknowledges that it has read, understands, and agrees to be bound by the terms and conditions set forth in Article III.
