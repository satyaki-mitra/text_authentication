Title: The Impact of Physical Activity on Mental Health

Physical activity plays a significant role in promoting mental health and overall well-being. Regular exercise stimulates the production of endorphins, often referred to as 'feel-good' hormones, which help reduce feelings of stress, anxiety, and depression. Engaging in physical activities also promotes better sleep quality, improves self-esteem, and boosts cognitive functions such as memory and concentration. Furthermore, regular exercise can provide a healthy distraction from daily stressors, helping individuals to maintain a positive outlook. It is recommended that adults engage in at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity or 75 minutes of high-intensity physical activity each week for optimal mental health benefits.
