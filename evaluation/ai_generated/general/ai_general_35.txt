The subject of climate change, a pressing global concern, has garnered widespread attention in recent years. Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperatures and typical weather patterns, primarily due to human activities such as burning fossil fuels and deforestation. These activities lead to increased levels of greenhouse gases in the Earth's atmosphere, particularly carbon dioxide and methane, which trap heat and cause global temperatures to rise, a phenomenon known as global warming. The effects of climate change are far-reaching and diverse, encompassing rising sea levels, more frequent and severe weather events like hurricanes and droughts, melting polar ice caps, and shifts in ecosystems and wildlife populations. Addressing climate change requires concerted international efforts to reduce greenhouse gas emissions, transition to renewable energy sources, improve energy efficiency, and promote sustainable agricultural practices.
