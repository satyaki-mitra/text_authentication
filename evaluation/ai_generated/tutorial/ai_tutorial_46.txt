Title: Guide to Creating Your First Website with WordPress

**Step 1: Setting Up a Domain and Hosting Account**

1. Choose a domain registrar like Namecheap, GoDaddy, or Bluehost. Purchase a domain name that represents your website's purpose.

2. If you didn't purchase hosting along with the domain, sign up for web hosting services such as Bluehost, SiteGround, or DreamHost. Ensure they offer one-click WordPress installation.

**Step 2: Installing WordPress**

1. Log in to your host's control panel (cPanel).

2. Look for the 'WordPress' icon, usually found under 'Software' or 'Autoinstallers'. Click on it and follow the instructions to install WordPress on your domain.

3. After installation, you will receive login credentials via email. Use these to access your WordPress dashboard at www.yourdomain.com/wp-admin.

**Step 3: Customizing Your Website**

1. Visit the WordPress Dashboard and explore its interface. Familiarize yourself with Menus, Pages, Posts, Comments, Appearance, Plugins, Users, and Settings.

2. Choose a theme from the Appearance > Themes section. You can either use a free theme or purchase a premium one. Click 'Activate' to apply the selected theme.

3. Customize your theme by navigating to Appearance > Customize. Here you can change various settings like site title, tagline, colors, background images, and more.

**Step 4: Adding Content**

1. Go to Pages > Add New to create a new page or post. Give it a title and write the content in the editor.

2. Use 'Blocks' to format your text, add images, videos, galleries, etc. You can also install additional blocks through Plugins > Add New > Search for desired block and click Install & Activate.

3. Once done, publish or update the page/post by clicking the Publish button in the top-right corner.
