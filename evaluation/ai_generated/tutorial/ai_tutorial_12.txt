Title: A Step-by-Step Guide for Beginners to Create an Email Account on Gmail

**Step 1: Open the Google Homepage**

Open your web browser (such as Chrome, Firefox, or Safari) and type in `www.google.com` in the address bar. Press Enter to navigate to the Google homepage.

**Step 2: Click on Gmail**

On the Google homepage, look for the "Gmail" icon located at the top-left corner of the screen. It is represented by a red 'envelope' symbol with a white 'p' inside it. Click on this icon to proceed.

**Step 3: Create an Account (If you don't have one)**

If you do not already have a Google account, click on "Create account" located at the bottom of the Gmail login page. This will redirect you to a new page where you can create your own Google account. Fill in all the required fields with the necessary information and follow the prompts to complete the registration process.

**Step 4: Sign In (If you already have a Google Account)**

If you already have a Google account, enter your email address (the one you used when creating the account) and password in the respective fields on the Gmail login page. Then click "Next" to proceed.

**Step 5: Verify Your Account (2-step Verification)**

For added security, Google may ask for a verification code sent to your phone or another method of verification based on your account settings. Follow the prompts to complete this step and access your Gmail account.

**Step 6: Get Familiar with the Gmail Interface**

Once you're signed in, you will be taken to the Gmail inbox where you can manage your emails. Familiarize yourself with the different sections such as:

- Compose (to write new emails)
- Inbox (for received emails)
- Stars and important (to prioritize emails)
- Sent mail (emails that you have sent)
- Spam (emails that are considered spam or junk)
