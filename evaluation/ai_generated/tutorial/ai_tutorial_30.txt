Title: A Beginner's Guide to Setting Up an Email Account Using Gmail

In this comprehensive guide, we will walk you through the process of setting up an email account using Gmail, a popular and user-friendly email service provided by Google. By the end of this tutorial, you should have your very own Gmail account and be able to send and receive emails confidently.

**Step 1: Creating a Google Account**
Before we can set up an email account using Gmail, you'll first need to create a Google Account, which will give you access to various Google services including Gmail.

1.1 Navigate your web browser to `google.com`.
1.2 Click on the "Sign In" button located at the top right corner of the page.
1.3 Below the sign-in fields, click on the option saying "Create account."
1.4 Fill in the required information for creating a Google Account, such as your name, email address (we will change this later), password, and birthdate. Make sure to read through and agree to Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
1.5 Complete the verification process by entering your phone number or providing an alternate email address. You may also need to confirm your identity with a CAPTCHA or by following additional steps provided by Google.

**Step 2: Changing Your Email Address**
Now that you have a Google Account, let's change the default email address associated with it and set up a new Gmail account.

2.1 Sign in to your Google Account using the credentials you just created.
2.2 Click on the profile picture or initial located at the top right corner of the screen. A dropdown menu will appear.
2.3 From the dropdown, select "Manage your Google Account."
2.4 On the left-hand side of the account management page, click on "Personal Info" and then select "Contact info."
2.5 Click on "Add phone or email" and select "Email address."
2.6 Enter a unique email address you'd like to use for your Gmail account (e.g., `yourname@gmail.com`), and click on "Next."
2.7 Complete the verification process by following the prompts, which may include checking your email inbox or phone for a verification code.
2.8 Once verified, you'll see your new email address displayed under the "Primary Email" section. Click on the three dots next to it and choose "Make default."
