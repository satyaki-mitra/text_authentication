Title: A Step-by-Step Guide to Drawing a Simple House for Beginners

Materials Needed:
1. Pencil and eraser
2. Paper
3. Colored pencils or paints (optional)

Steps:

1. **Draw the Base**:
   - Start by drawing a rectangle as the base of your house. This will be the foundation.

2. **Draw the Roof**:
   - Draw two triangles on opposite ends of the rectangle. The bottom edge of each triangle should touch one end of the rectangle. These will be the roofs of your house.

3. **Draw the Walls**:
   - Connect the top corners of the rectangular base and the triangles with straight lines to form the walls of your house.

4. **Add a Door**:
   - Draw a vertical line about halfway down the height of one of the walls. Above this line, draw a smaller rectangle or square for the door frame. Inside this frame, sketch out the shape of the door itself.

5. **Draw Windows**:
   - Sketch two small rectangles above the door on opposite walls to represent windows. If you prefer, you can also add more windows along the length of your house.

6. **Add Details**:
   - To make your house look more detailed and realistic, you can draw a roof peak at the top center by connecting the two triangular roofs with a line. You can also draw lines to represent bricks or shingles on the walls and roof.

7. **Draw a Chimney**:
   - On one of the roof peaks, draw a vertical rectangle to create a chimney. Add a few lines inside for a more intricate look.

8. **Erasing Guidelines**:
   - Carefully erase all unnecessary guidelines and any areas you want to refine.

9. (Optional) **Coloring**:
   - You can now color your house using colored pencils or paints. Feel free to be creative with the colors!

10. **Final Touches**:
    - Lastly, if desired, add small details like a garden, a pathway, or some trees around your house to make it more lively and appealing.
