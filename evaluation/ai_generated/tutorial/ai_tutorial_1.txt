Title: A Comprehensive Guide for Beginners: Setting Up an FTP Client on Windows 10

This tutorial will guide you through the process of setting up an FTP (File Transfer Protocol) client on your Windows 10 computer. An FTP client allows you to transfer files between your local computer and a remote server, which is essential for web development or managing files on a remote host. We'll be using FileZilla, a free, open-source FTP client that offers a user-friendly interface.

**Step 1: Download and Install FileZilla**

1.1 Visit the official FileZilla website (https://filezilla-project.org/) to download the appropriate version for Windows.
1.2 Click on the "Download FileZilla Client" button, and save the file to your preferred location.
1.3 Once the download is complete, run the installer by double-clicking the downloaded file.
1.4 Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation process.

**Step 2: Open FileZilla**

2.1 Navigate to the FileZilla application in your Start menu or desktop shortcut and click to open it.

**Step 3: Set Up Your FTP Site**

3.1 In the "Site Manager" window, click on "File" from the top menu, then select "Site Manager."
3.2 Click on the "New Site" button at the bottom of the window to create a new connection profile.
3.3 Give your site a recognizable name in the "Host" field (e.g., MyWebsite).
3.4 Enter your FTP server's address, typically provided by your web host, in the "Host" field (e.g., ftp.example.com).
3.5 In the "Port" field, enter the default FTP port number 21 unless otherwise specified by your web host.
3.6 Enter your FTP username and password in their respective fields. These are also provided by your web host.
3.7 Ensure the "Logon Type" is set to "Normal."
3.8 Click on the "Connect" button at the bottom of the window to test the connection. If successful, you will be connected to your FTP server.
