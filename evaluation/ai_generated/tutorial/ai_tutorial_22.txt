Title: A Step-by-Step Guide to Setting Up a Basic Blog using WordPress

**Step 1: Choosing a Web Hosting Provider and Domain Name**

1.1 Research various web hosting providers such as Bluehost, SiteGround, or HostGator, to find one that suits your needs in terms of cost, features, and reliability.

1.2 Purchase a hosting plan from your chosen provider. This usually includes a free domain name for the first year. If not, you can register a domain separately through services like GoDaddy or Namecheap.

**Step 2: Installing WordPress**

2.1 Log in to your web host's control panel (cPanel). This is typically provided by your hosting provider upon account creation.

2.2 Look for the "Softaculous Apps" section and click on it. Alternatively, you might find a "WordPress" icon directly within cPanel.

2.3 Click on "Install Now" or similar button to initiate the WordPress installation process.

2.4 Follow the on-screen instructions, entering your domain name, site name, username, password, and email address when prompted. Make sure to choose a strong password for security reasons.

2.5 After clicking "Install," WordPress will be installed on your domain. Once completed, you'll receive an admin email containing login credentials.

**Step 3: Accessing Your New Blog and Customizing It**

3.1 Navigate to your website's front-end by typing your domain name into a web browser. You should see the default WordPress theme.

3.2 Log in to your WordPress dashboard using the credentials provided in the email from Step 2.5. This can be done by appending "/wp-admin" to your domain (e.g., `yourdomain.com/wp-admin`).

3.3 Familiarize yourself with the WordPress dashboard layout, particularly the left-hand menu which contains various settings and customization options.

**Step 4: Choosing a Theme and Customizing Your Blog's Appearance**

4.1 To change your blog's theme, hover over "Appearance" in the left-hand menu, then click on "Themes."

4.2 Click on "Add New" to browse through available free themes.
