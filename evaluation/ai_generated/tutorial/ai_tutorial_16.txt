Title: A Step-by-Step Guide for Beginners to Set Up an Email Account on Gmail

**Step 1: Open a Web Browser**

First, open your preferred web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.) on your computer or mobile device.

**Step 2: Navigate to the Gmail Homepage**

In the address bar at the top of your web browser, type www.gmail.com and hit Enter. This will take you to the Gmail homepage where you can create a new account if you don't have one or sign in if you already have an account.

**Step 3: Sign Up for a New Account (If Necessary)**

If you don't have a Google/Gmail account yet, click on "Create account" located at the bottom of the page. Fill out the required information such as first name, last name, username, password, and date of birth. Make sure to provide accurate information as it is necessary for account verification.

**Step 4: Verify Your Email Address**

Once you've filled out all the required fields, click "Next." Google will then send a verification code to your phone number or email address (you can choose either). Enter this code in the designated field and click "Verify."

**Step 5: Set Up Account Recovery Information**

After verifying your account, you'll be prompted to provide additional recovery information such as another email address, a mobile phone number, or security questions. This step is essential for recovering your account in case you forget your password.

**Step 6: Complete the Setup Process**

Follow the remaining steps on the setup page to personalize your Gmail account by adding your profile picture, choosing your preferred language, and setting up any additional features such as Google Drive storage or YouTube access.

**Step 7: Sign In to Your New Gmail Account**

Now that you've set up your new account, sign in using your email address and password at the top-right corner of the Gmail homepage.
