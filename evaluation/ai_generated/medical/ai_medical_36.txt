Title: A Case Study on the Successful Management of Refractory Status Epilepticus with Levetiracetam and Ketogenic Diet in a Pediatric Patient

Abstract

Background: Refractory status epilepticus (RSE) is a critical neurological emergency, characterized by persistent seizures that are unresponsive to conventional antiepileptic drugs. The management of RSE remains challenging due to its high morbidity and mortality rates. This case report presents an atypical approach to the treatment of a pediatric patient with RSE, combining Levetiracetam and the Ketogenic Diet, resulting in successful seizure control.

Case Presentation: A 7-year-old male presented to the emergency department with a 3-day history of recurrent generalized tonic-clonic seizures. Despite aggressive pharmacological intervention with midazolam and phenytoin, the patient's seizures persisted, meeting the criteria for RSE. Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) revealed bilateral hippocampal sclerosis, suggesting a potential structural etiology. Due to the refractory nature of the seizures and the patient's underlying neurological condition, a decision was made to initiate adjunctive therapy with Levetiracetam and implement a ketogenic diet.

Within 48 hours of initiating Levetiracetam and the ketogenic diet, the patient experienced a significant reduction in seizure frequency. The ketogenic diet was continued over the subsequent months, with regular adjustments to the medication regimen to maintain seizure control. After three months of combined therapy, the patient was seizure-free and able to be weaned off all antiepileptic medications except Levetiracetam, which continues to provide sustained seizure control.

Discussion: This case report highlights an uncommon yet effective approach in managing RSE. The combination of Levetiracetam and the ketogenic diet offers a potential alternative for patients with refractory epilepsy who have not responded to conventional treatment options. Further research is warranted to explore the mechanisms underlying this therapeutic synergy and to establish guidelines for the safe and effective use of these treatments in the management of RSE.

Keywords: Refractory Status Epilepticus, Levetiracetam, Ketogenic Diet, Pediatric Epilepsy, Adjunctive Therapy
