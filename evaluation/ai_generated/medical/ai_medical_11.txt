Title: A Case Study on Successful Management of Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome Coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2) Infection in a High-Risk Immunocompromised Patient

Abstract

Background: SARS-CoV-2, the etiological agent of coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19), poses significant threats to vulnerable populations, including immunocompromised individuals. This case report highlights an unusual and challenging scenario where a high-risk immunocompromised patient successfully recovered from severe COVID-19.

Case Presentation: A 60-year-old male with a history of chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) undergoing chemotherapy presented to the emergency department with fever, cough, and dyspnea for three days. The patient had been recently hospitalized for an acute CLL flare and was receiving rituximab at the time of COVID-19 diagnosis. Upon admission, a chest X-ray revealed bilateral pulmonary infiltrates consistent with viral pneumonia. Reverse transcription polymerase chain reaction (RT-PCR) test confirmed SARS-CoV-2 infection. The patient was immediately isolated and started on empirical antiviral therapy, including remdesivir, dexamethasone, and prophylactic enoxaparin. Given the patient's immunocompromised status, tocilizumab was also initiated.

Diagnostic Approach: The diagnostic approach involved a comprehensive history taking, physical examination, laboratory tests, and imaging studies. RT-PCR testing of nasopharyngeal swabs was performed for SARS-CoV-2 detection. Complete blood count (CBC), complete metabolic panel (CMP), and lactate dehydrogenase (LDH) were utilized to monitor the patient's condition. A chest X-ray and computed tomography pulmonary angiogram (CTPA) were conducted for radiological assessment of lung involvement and exclusion of other etiologies, such as pulmonary embolism.

Treatment: The patient received a combination of antiviral therapy, immunomodulatory agents, and supportive care during hospitalization. Remdesivir was administered intravenously for five days, followed by dexamethasone at a dose of 6 mg daily for ten days. Tocilizumab was given as a single 8 mg/kg dose on day three due to the patient's immunocompromised status and severe COVID-19 presentation. The supportive care included oxygen therapy, fluid management, and nutritional support.
