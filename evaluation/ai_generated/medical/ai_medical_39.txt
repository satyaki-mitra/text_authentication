Title: A Case Study on Successful Treatment of Refractory Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumor (GIST) with Imatinib Mesylate

Abstract

Background: Gastrointestinal stromal tumors (GISTs) are the most common mesenchymal neoplasms of the gastrointestinal tract, accounting for approximately 1% of all gastric malignancies. Imatinib Mesylate, a tyrosine kinase inhibitor, has revolutionized the treatment landscape of GIST due to its targeted action against the c-Kit and platelet-derived growth factor receptor (PDGFR) alpha/beta. However, cases of refractory GIST to initial imatinib therapy remain challenging.

Case Presentation: A 62-year-old female presented with a one-year history of progressive abdominal pain and weight loss. Endoscopic ultrasound revealed a 5cm x 4cm mass in the antrum of the stomach, consistent with GIST based on cytological examination. The patient was initially treated with imatinib at a dose of 400mg daily. However, after six months, CT scans showed disease progression with multiple liver metastases. Given the refractory nature of the tumor to initial imatinib therapy, the patient was considered for second-line treatment options.

Diagnostic Approach: The patient underwent a comprehensive workup, including CT scans, MRI, and cytogenetic analysis of the primary and metastatic lesions. The tumor was found to harbor a PDGFRα-activating mutation (D842V), suggesting potential resistance to imatinib.

Treatment: Given the resistant nature of the GIST, the patient was switched to Regorafenib, an oral multikinase inhibitor, approved for the treatment of refractory GIST. The initial dose was 160mg daily, with a dose adjustment based on tolerability and efficacy assessments.

Outcomes: After three months of Regorafenib therapy, a follow-up CT scan showed significant reduction in the size of both the primary tumor and liver metastases. The patient reported improved symptoms and quality of life. After one year of treatment, the patient continues to show stable disease with minimal side effects, demonstrating the efficacy of Regorafenib in managing refractory GIST.
