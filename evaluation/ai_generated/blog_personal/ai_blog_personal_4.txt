Title: Finding Joy in the Midst of Change - My Journey Through Uncertain Times

Hey there, friends! I hope this little blog finds you well. Today, I wanted to share some thoughts on a topic that's been close to my heart lately - finding joy amid change and uncertainty.

As many of you know, life has a funny way of throwing us curveballs when we least expect it. For me, the last couple of years have been a whirlwind of moves, new jobs, and unexpected twists and turns. I won't lie; there were times when it felt like I was barely keeping my head above water. But looking back now, I can see that even in the midst of chaos, there were moments of pure, unadulterated joy.

One such moment came during a particularly grueling move across the country. As boxes piled up around me and the movers seemed to be leaving behind more than they were taking with them, I found myself on the floor in the middle of our new living room, surrounded by chaos. My sweet dog, Winston, was lying next to me, panting heavily from the exertion of the day. In that moment, as the sun streamed through the window and bathed us in a golden glow, I felt an overwhelming sense of peace.

In another instance, I found myself starting a new job with little experience or confidence. The first few weeks were intimidating to say the least, but one afternoon, during a particularly tough day, I was working on a project that required me to think creatively and outside the box. I felt my heart race as an idea took shape in my mind, and I knew that I had created something truly special. In that moment, the hard work and self-doubt melted away, and I was filled with a sense of pride and purpose that I hadn't experienced in a long time.

These moments may seem small in the grand scheme of things, but they were the reminders I needed to keep going during challenging times. They served as a reminder that even when life feels overwhelming, there is always beauty to be found if we take a moment to pause and look for it.

So why am I sharing all of this with you today? Well, because I believe that we all need reminding from time to time that life isn't just about the big moments - the weddings, the promotions, the milestones. It's also about those tiny, seemingly insignificant moments that come and go in the blink of an eye. Those moments that often slip through the cracks when we're too busy stressing about what's next.
