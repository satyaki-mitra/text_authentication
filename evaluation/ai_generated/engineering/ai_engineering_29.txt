Title: Performance Analysis and Recommendations for the Optimization of Data Center Cooling Systems in Modern Server Farms

1. Introduction

This technical report aims to analyze the current state of data center cooling systems in modern server farms, identify performance bottlenecks, and propose recommendations for system optimization. The focus is on improving energy efficiency, reducing operational costs, and enhancing overall system reliability.

2. System Overview

The data center cooling system under investigation consists of a series of air-cooled computer rooms equipped with precision air conditioning units, hot/cold aisle containment, and economizers for outdoor air utilization. The primary goal is to maintain optimal temperature and humidity levels within the server racks while minimizing energy consumption.

3. Specifications

The system specifications are as follows:

- Number of computer rooms: 10
- Precision air conditioning units per room: 5
- Total number of air conditioning units: 50
- Hot/cold aisle containment ratio: 1:1
- Economizer operation temperature range: 50°F to 95°F (outdoor air) and 72°F to 84°F (indoor air)

4. Performance Analysis

Upon thorough examination of the data center's performance, it was observed that energy consumption peaked during periods when economizer operation was not possible due to outdoor temperature extremes. Moreover, hot/cold aisle containment was not consistently enforced, leading to mixing of heated and cooled air within server racks.

4.1 Energy Consumption Analysis

During the summer months, energy consumption increased by an average of 20% due to the higher reliance on mechanical cooling rather than outdoor air economization. This increase in energy consumption resulted in higher operational costs and a less environmentally friendly operation.

4.2 Temperature and Humidity Analysis

Temperature and humidity levels within the server racks were generally well-controlled; however, there were instances where hot/cold aisle containment was inadequate, leading to variations in temperature and humidity readings across different sections of the data center.

5. Design Constraints

The following design constraints were identified during the performance analysis:

- Outdoor air economization is limited by extreme temperatures
- Hot/cold aisle containment can be compromised due to poor implementation or maintenance
- Precision air conditioning units may require periodic maintenance and replacement, increasing costs
