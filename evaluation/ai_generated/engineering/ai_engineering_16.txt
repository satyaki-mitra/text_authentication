Title: Performance Analysis and Optimization of the Proposed Data Center Cooling System Design

1. Introduction

This technical report presents an in-depth analysis of the proposed data center cooling system design, aiming to optimize energy efficiency while maintaining optimal temperature and humidity levels for server operation. The following sections outline the specifications, performance analysis, design constraints, and recommendations for the proposed system.

2. System Specifications

The proposed data center cooling system employs a two-phase evaporative cooling (TPEC) solution with direct and indirect economizer cycles. Key components include chiller units, heat exchangers, cooling towers, and air handling units (AHUs). The TPEC system is designed to utilize free cooling during cooler outdoor temperatures and switch to mechanical cooling when necessary.

3. Performance Analysis

The performance of the proposed cooling system was evaluated using simulation software, considering factors such as temperature, humidity, and power consumption. The results indicate that the TPEC system can effectively maintain server room conditions within the acceptable range (20-25°C) during 80% of the year with free cooling alone. During warmer months or periods of high heat load, the mechanical cooling component will supplement the free cooling to ensure consistent temperature and humidity control.

4. Design Constraints

The following design constraints were considered in the development of the TPEC system:

a) Energy Efficiency: The cooling system must minimize energy consumption while maintaining optimal server conditions.
b) Reliability: The system should be designed to maintain operation during power outages or equipment failures.
c) Scalability: The solution should allow for future expansion as data center capacity grows.
d) Space Utilization: The cooling system components should optimize available space within the data center.
e) Cost-Effectiveness: The proposed design must provide a cost-effective solution that offers long-term savings on energy costs and maintenance.

5. Recommendations

Based on the performance analysis and design constraints, the following recommendations have been made for the optimization of the TPEC system:

a) Implement an advanced control system to optimize the operation of chiller units, cooling towers, and AHUs based on real-time data from temperature sensors and humidity probes. This will ensure efficient energy usage and consistent server room conditions.
b) Install a battery backup system to maintain operation during power outages or equipment failures, enhancing overall system reliability.
c) Incorporate modular components into the design, allowing for easy expansion of the cooling system as data center capacity grows.
