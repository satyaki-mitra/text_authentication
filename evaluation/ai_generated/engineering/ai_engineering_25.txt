Title: Performance Analysis and Design Optimization of a Decentralized Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Microgrid System for Remote Off-Grid Communities

1. Introduction

This report presents an analysis of a decentralized solar photovoltaic (PV) microgrid system designed for off-grid communities in remote regions. The proposed system aims to provide a sustainable, cost-effective, and scalable solution for electricity access, reducing reliance on fossil fuels and promoting energy independence.

2. System Design Overview

The decentralized PV microgrid consists of individual solar PV arrays, energy storage systems (ESS), and load management units (LMU) interconnected to form a distributed network. Each PV array is connected to an ESS and LMU, enabling local power generation, storage, and distribution.

3. Technical Specifications

Each PV module has a peak power output of 250W, with a total capacity of 10 kW for each decentralized system. The ESS comprises lithium-ion batteries with an energy storage capacity of 40 kWh. The LMU integrates solar charge controllers, inverters, and load management units to manage the flow of power within the system.

4. Performance Analysis

The performance analysis was conducted using PVWatts, a tool developed by the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL). Assuming an average daily sunlight hours of 5.67 hours and a solar irradiance of 5.38 kWh/m²/day, the estimated annual energy production (AEP) for each 10kW system is approximately 24,900 kWh.

Considering a typical annual energy consumption of 18,000 kWh for an off-grid household, each system would be capable of meeting the energy demands of three such households. The remaining excess energy can be stored in the ESS for peak load periods or distributed to neighboring communities.

5. Design Constraints

The design constraints include:

- Topography and geographical location: Ensuring optimal solar panel tilt angles and orientation to maximize solar irradiation.
- Weather conditions: Selecting robust materials that can withstand extreme temperatures, humidity, and wind speeds in the target regions.
- Energy storage capacity: Balancing the storage capacity with cost, durability, and safety concerns.
- Load management: Developing efficient algorithms to balance energy demand and supply, minimize power loss, and ensure system stability.
