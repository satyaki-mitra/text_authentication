Title: Performance Analysis and Design Optimization of a Distributed Energy Storage System for Grid Stabilization

Executive Summary:

This technical report presents an analysis of the design and performance of a distributed energy storage system (DESS) implemented for grid stabilization purposes. The system, comprising lithium-ion battery packs and power conversion systems, is strategically positioned across various grid nodes to provide reactive power support, frequency regulation, and voltage control services.

System Design:

The DESS consists of 126 modular battery units, each with a capacity of 500 kWh, connected in parallel through bidirectional DC-DC converters. The total system capacity is 63 MWh, with a voltage rating of 800 V DC and an operating temperature range between -20°C and +40°C. Each battery unit is equipped with a liquid cooling system to ensure thermal management during high discharge rates or ambient temperature variations.

The power conversion systems are designed for maximum efficiency, with a conversion loss of less than 3%. They facilitate bidirectional energy flow between the battery packs and the grid, enabling both charging and discharging operations. Each conversion system is connected to two battery units, providing redundancy in case of component failure.

Performance Analysis:

Under normal operating conditions, the DESS is designed to respond rapidly (within milliseconds) to grid disturbances such as voltage sags or swells, frequency deviations, and reactive power imbalances. The system's response time has been validated through simulation studies and laboratory tests, demonstrating a high level of performance.

To quantify the DESS's contribution to grid stability, we analyzed its ability to provide various ancillary services. In particular, the system is able to:

1. Frequency regulation: The DESS can quickly adjust its power output to compensate for load changes and generation outages, thereby maintaining a stable grid frequency within the acceptable range (±0.5 Hz).
2. Voltage control: By injecting or absorbing reactive power as needed, the DESS helps maintain the grid voltage at the prescribed level (13.8 kV for our case study).
3. Reactive power support: The system can provide rapid reactive power support during voltage sags, thereby reducing the magnitude and duration of the event.
