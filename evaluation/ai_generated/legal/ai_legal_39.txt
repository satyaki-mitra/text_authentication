Title: Data Protection Policy - Confidentiality Clause

1. Introduction

This Confidentiality Clause (the "Clause") is an integral part of any agreement between the Parties (collectively referred to as the "Agreement"), and is intended to protect confidential information exchanged between them. The terms used in this Clause shall have the meanings set forth in the Agreement, or as otherwise defined herein.

2. Scope and Duration

2.1 This Clause shall apply to all Confidential Information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether before, on, or after the effective date of this Agreement.

2.2 The obligations under this Clause shall survive termination or expiration of the Agreement for a period of five (5) years unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Parties.

3. Definition of Confidential Information

3.1 For the purposes of this Clause, "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party that is not generally known to the public or to which the Receiving Party would have limited access in the normal course of business. Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to, technical data, trade secrets, and business, financial, and customer information.

4. Obligations of the Receiving Party

4.1 The Receiving Party agrees to use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing its obligations under the Agreement and shall not disclose such Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, except as required by law or a valid order of a court or other governmental body.

4.2 The Receiving Party shall take reasonable measures to protect the secrecy of and avoid unauthorized disclosure or use of the Confidential Information. Such measures shall include, but are not limited to, maintaining the confidentiality of the Confidential Information, restricting access to the Confidential Information to those employees who need such access for the performance of their duties under the Agreement, and implementing appropriate physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to secure the Confidential Information.

5.1.1 Is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the Receiving Party;

5.1.2 Was in the possession of the Receiving Party prior to receipt from the Disclosing Party;
