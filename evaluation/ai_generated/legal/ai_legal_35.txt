Case Title: Smith v. Johnson

Court: Supreme Court of the State of New York, County of Albany

Docket Number: 20-CV-12345

Judge: Honorable Jane Doe

Date Decided: January 1, 2022

Parties:
Plaintiff: John Smith
Defendant: Mary Johnson

Facts:
The Plaintiff, John Smith, alleges that the Defendant, Mary Johnson, breached a contractual agreement by failing to fulfill her obligations under the terms of an employment contract. Specifically, Mr. Smith claims that Ms. Johnson failed to meet performance targets and did not adhere to the agreed-upon work schedule.

Procedural History:
The parties entered into a written employment contract on March 1, 2020, which outlined their respective rights and obligations. Mr. Smith filed this action against Ms. Johnson on September 1, 2020, asserting breach of contract claims. The Defendant filed an answer denying the allegations and asserted various affirmative defenses.

Legal Clause:
Whereas, the Plaintiff, John Smith, contends that the Defendant, Mary Johnson, has breached a valid employment contract by failing to perform her obligations as outlined in the agreement entered into on March 1, 2020; and

Whereas, the Defendant denies these allegations and asserts various affirmative defenses;

Now, therefore, it is hereby ordered that:

1. The Plaintiff's motion for summary judgment on his breach of contract claim is granted, and the Defendant is found to have breached the employment contract entered into on March 1, 2020.

2. The Defendant is directed to pay damages to the Plaintiff in an amount to be determined at a separate trial, based upon the evidence presented regarding lost wages, benefits, and other costs incurred as a result of the breach.

3. This order does not preclude either party from seeking additional relief with regard to any remaining issues or counterclaims.

4. This matter is adjourned for a trial on damages to be held on a date to be determined by the Court, upon the mutual agreement of the parties.
