Title: Data Processing Agreement (DPA) - Clause 3: Data Subject Rights

1. **Scope**

This Clause 3 (Data Subject Rights) is a part of the Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") between the Controller, identified as [Controller's Name] with its registered office at [Controller's Address] ("Controller"), and the Processor, identified as [Processor's Name] with its registered office at [Processor's Address] ("Processor").

2. **Data Subject Rights**

2.1 The Processor shall, to the extent legally permitted and technically feasible, assist the Controller in fulfilling its obligations under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (General Data Protection Regulation) ("GDPR") and any other applicable data protection laws.

2.2 The Processor shall, at the Controller's request, provide reasonable assistance to enable the Controller to respond to:

   2.2.1 requests from data subjects seeking to exercise their rights under the GDPR (including the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, and data portability); and

   2.2.2 any other correspondence, notification or complaint received from a data protection authority.

3. **Cooperation and Assistance**

3.1 The Processor shall, at the Controller's cost, provide all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with its obligations under this Agreement, the GDPR, and any other applicable data protection laws.

3.2 The Processor shall promptly notify the Controller if it receives a request or notification that relates directly to the processing of Personal Data by the Processor under this Agreement.

4. **Data Protection Impact Assessment**

The Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to the Controller with any data protection impact assessments, and prior consultations with supervisory authorities or other competent data protection authorities, as required by the GDPR.

5. **Subprocessors**

If the Processor engages another processor (a "Subprocessor") for carrying out specific processing activities on behalf of the Controller, the Processor shall:

   5.1 inform the Controller of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of Subprocessors; and

   5.2 ensure that the Subprocessor agrees to comply with the same data protection obligations as set out in this Agreement.
