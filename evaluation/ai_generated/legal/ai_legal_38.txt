Case Summary: Smith v. Johnson

Court: Supreme Court of New York, County of Albany

Docket Number: A-123456

Judgment Date: March 1, 2023

Parties:
1. Plaintiff: John P. Smith (Plaintiff)
2. Defendant: Mary S. Johnson (Defendant)

Facts:
The plaintiff, John P. Smith, brought an action against the defendant, Mary S. Johnson, for breach of contract and tortious interference with a business relationship arising from a commercial lease agreement. The dispute stemmed from the defendant's alleged failure to fulfill her obligations under the lease and her actions that purportedly harmed Mr. Smith's business relations with the property's other tenants.

Issues:
1. Whether the defendant breached the commercial lease agreement by failing to meet her contractual obligations, resulting in damages to the plaintiff.
2. Whether the defendant's actions constitute tortious interference with a business relationship and caused injury to the plaintiff's business relations with other tenants on the property.

Holdings:
1. The Court found that the defendant had indeed breached the commercial lease agreement by not fulfilling her contractual obligations, causing the plaintiff actual and substantial damages. The court ordered the defendant to pay compensatory damages in the amount of $500,000 to the plaintiff.
2. Regarding tortious interference with a business relationship, the Court ruled that the defendant's actions were malicious and intentionally aimed at harming the plaintiff's business relationships on the property. The court ordered the defendant to pay an additional $300,000 in punitive damages to the plaintiff as a deterrent for similar behavior in the future.
