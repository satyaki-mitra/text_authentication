Clause: Indemnity and Hold Harmless Agreement

In this Agreement, the Parties hereby agree as follows:

1. Definition of Terms: For the purposes of this Agreement, "Parties" shall refer collectively to [Party A] and [Party B]. "Claims" shall include any claims, demands, actions, damages, costs, liabilities, settlements, losses, judgments, awards, or expenses, including attorneys' fees.

2. Indemnity: Each Party (the "Indemnifying Party") agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other Party (the "Indemnified Party") from any Claims arising out of or resulting from the Indemnifying Party's breach of this Agreement, its negligence, or its willful misconduct.

3. Scope: This indemnity extends to all claims that may arise in connection with the subject matter of this Agreement, including but not limited to claims arising from intellectual property rights, contractual obligations, tortious acts, and statutory violations.

4. Survival: This indemnity shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement.

5. Exclusivity: The Indemnified Party shall have the exclusive right to control the defense and settlement of any Claims subject to indemnification under this Agreement, provided that the Indemnifying Party is given reasonable notice before any such settlement is executed.
