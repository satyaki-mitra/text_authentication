Title: Data Processing Agreement - Clause 4: Data Subject Rights

4. Data Subject Rights

4.1 Obligations Regarding Data Subject Requests

4.1.1 Data Controller (hereinafter "Controller") shall have the right to instruct Data Processor (hereinafter "Processor") to take reasonable steps to respond to requests from Data Subjects (hereinafter "Data Subjects") in relation to their rights under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation or "GDPR").

4.1.2 Processor shall, to the extent legally permitted and technically feasible, assist Controller in fulfilling its obligations under Articles 12 to 22 of GDPR in relation to Data Subjects' requests for:

   (a) Access to Personal Data (Article 15);
   (b) Rectification of Personal Data (Article 16);
   (c) Erasure of Personal Data (Article 17);
   (d) Restriction of processing of Personal Data (Article 18);
   (e) Objection to processing of Personal Data (Article 21); and
   (f) Data Portability (Article 20).

4.1.3 Processor shall, upon Controller's reasonable request, provide information regarding the measures taken in accordance with this Clause 4.1.2.

4.2 Notification of Data Subject Requests

4.2.1 Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay if it receives a request from a Data Subject exercising any of their rights under GDPR, unless EU or Member State law to which the Processor is subject prohibits such notification on important grounds of public interest.

4.2.2 Notification under this Clause 4.2.1 shall include:

   (a) The nature of the request;
   (b) Any action taken by Processor in response to the request; and
   (c) A copy of the Data Subject's request, unless the request was for rectification.

4.3 Assistance with Data Protection Impact Assessments and Prior Consultation

4.3.1 Upon Controller's reasonable request, Processor shall provide assistance to Controller in conducting data protection impact assessments (DPIAs) and consultations with supervisory authorities under Article 35 and 36 of GDPR, respectively, where such DPIAs or consultations are required by law.
