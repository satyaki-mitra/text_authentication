Title: Data Processing Agreement ("DPA")

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on [Insert Date] between [Your Company Name], a company incorporated under the laws of [Your Country] with its registered office at [Your Address] (hereinafter referred to as "Data Controller"), and [Client's Company Name], a company incorporated under the laws of [Client's Country] with its registered office at [Client's Address] (hereinafter referred to as "Data Processor").

WHEREAS:

1. The Parties have entered into an agreement for Data Processing Services ("Agreement") pursuant to which the Data Processor shall process Personal Data on behalf of the Data Controller;
2. Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (General Data Protection Regulation) ("GDPR") sets out requirements for the processing of Personal Data;
3. The Parties wish to set out their respective obligations and rights with respect to the processing of Personal Data in accordance with the GDPR.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out in the GDPR or, if not defined therein, the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 Capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined herein shall have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

2. Processing of Personal Data

2.1 The Data Processor shall process the Personal Data only for the purposes set out in the Agreement and in accordance with the written instructions of the Data Controller.

2.2 The Data Processor shall promptly inform the Data Controller if, in its opinion, any instruction given by the Data Controller infringes the GDPR or other applicable laws.

3. Rights and Obligations of the Parties

3.1 The Data Controller warrants that it has all necessary permissions to transfer the Personal Data to the Data Processor for processing in accordance with this Agreement, and that the Personal Data has been collected and processed in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

3.2 The Data Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk associated with the processing of the Personal Data.
