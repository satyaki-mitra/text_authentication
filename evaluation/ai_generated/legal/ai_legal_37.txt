Case Title: Smith v. Johnson

Court of Appeals, State of New York

Jurisdiction: Supreme Court, County of Albany

Docket Number: A-678910

Case Summary:

In the matter of Smith v. Johnson, the plaintiff, Mr. John Smith (hereafter referred to as "Plaintiff"), brings forth an action against the defendant, Ms. Jane Johnson (hereafter referred to as "Defendant"). The Plaintiff alleges that the Defendant breached a contractual agreement entered into on March 1st, 20XX, by failing to fulfill her obligations under said agreement.

The contract in question, specifically Article III, Section 4, stipulates that the Defendant was obligated to deliver goods of specified quantity and quality to the Plaintiff. The Plaintiff asserts that the Defendant failed to meet these requirements, causing damages to the Plaintiff's business operations.

The Plaintiff seeks compensatory damages in the amount of $100,000, along with attorney's fees and court costs. The Plaintiff requests a trial by jury.

This case is currently pending before the Honorable Judge Robert Davis in the Supreme Court of New York, County of Albany.
