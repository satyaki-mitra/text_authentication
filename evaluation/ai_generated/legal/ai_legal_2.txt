Title: Data Processing Agreement - Clause 8: Data Subject Rights

8. Data Subject Rights

8.1 Obligations Regarding Data Subject Requests

8.1.1 Data Controller (hereinafter "Controller") shall have the right to request, and Data Processor (hereinafter "Processor") shall promptly comply with any reasonable requests from the Controller with respect to accessing, correcting, deleting, or restricting the processing of Personal Data, which include but are not limited to:

  8.1.1.1 Right of Access: Providing the Controller with a copy of the Personal Data being processed and related information as required under applicable data protection laws;

  8.1.1.2 Right to Rectification: Correcting inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data at the Controller's request, subject to any verification of the accuracy of the new data;

  8.1.1.3 Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Deleting Personal Data at the Controller's request, unless Processor is required by applicable law to retain such data;

  8.1.1.4 Right to Restriction of Processing: Restricting the processing of Personal Data at the Controller's request, for example, while the accuracy of the data is being verified or an objection to processing is being resolved; and

  8.1.1.5 Right to Data Portability: Providing the Controller, or a third party designated by the Controller, with a copy of the Personal Data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, subject to technical feasibility.

8.2 Notification of Data Subject Requests

8.2.1 Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay upon receiving a request from a Data Subject exercising any of their rights under applicable data protection laws (hereinafter "Data Subject Request"). Such notification shall include all relevant information about the request and steps taken in response, if any.

8.3 Assistance with Data Subject Requests

8.3.1 Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to Controller in fulfilling its obligations under this Clause 8, including by:

  8.3.1.1 Providing the Controller with information and cooperation necessary for the Controller to respond to a Data Subject Request;

  8.3.1.2 Implementing reasonable measures to verify the identity of a Data Subject who submits a request under this Clause 8, if requested by the Controller; and

  8.3.1.3 Providing the Controller with information regarding Personal Data that is the subject of a Data Subject Request, as well as any available information about the origin of such data, if known to Processor.
