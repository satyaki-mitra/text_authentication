Clause: Indemnification and Hold Harmless Agreement

In this Agreement, the Indemnifying Party (hereinafter referred to as "Indemnifier") agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Indemnified Party (hereinafter referred to as "Indemnitee"), its affiliates, officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, losses, liabilities, damages, expenses, or costs (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or in connection with the Indemnifier's breach or alleged breach of this Agreement, violation of any law, rule, or regulation, or negligence or willful misconduct.

Case Summary: Smith v. Johnson (2021)

In the case of Smith v. Johnson (2021), the plaintiff, Mr. Smith, brought a lawsuit against the defendant, Mr. Johnson, alleging that Johnson breached an Indemnification and Hold Harmless Agreement by failing to indemnify and hold harmless Smith from third-party claims arising out of a business transaction between them. The court found in favor of Johnson, ruling that he had not breached the agreement because the alleged third-party claims did not arise out of or in connection with the Indemnifying Party's (Johnson) breach or alleged breach of this Agreement, violation of any law, rule, or regulation, or negligence or willful misconduct. The court further held that Johnson was only obligated to indemnify and hold harmless Smith for claims arising from the specific provisions outlined in the agreement, and not any other potential claims against Smith.
