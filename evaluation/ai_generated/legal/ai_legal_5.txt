Case Title: Smith v. Johnson

Court: Supreme Court of New York, County of Albany

Docket Number: 2021-CV-04567

Judgment Date: March 31, 2022

Clerk's Transcript:

In the matter before this Honorable Court, Plaintiff John Smith (hereinafter "Plaintiff"), by counsel, brings an action against Defendant Jane Johnson (hereinafter "Defendant") for breach of contract and negligence arising from a vehicle accident that occurred on January 15, 2022.

The material facts as presented by the Plaintiff in his complaint are as follows: On said date, Defendant was operating a motor vehicle at reckless speeds, resulting in an accident that caused significant property damage to Plaintiff's vehicle and personal injuries to the Plaintiff. The Plaintiff alleges that the Defendant breached their duty of care as a responsible driver by operating the vehicle in such a manner and further asserts that this conduct constitutes negligence under New York General Obligations Law § 11-102.

The Plaintiff seeks compensatory damages for both his property damage and personal injuries, as well as punitive damages to deter similar behavior from the Defendant and others in the future. The Plaintiff requests a trial by jury on all issues of fact and demands judgment against the Defendant in an amount to be determined by said jury.

This Court finds that the Plaintiff has sufficiently pleaded a claim for breach of contract and negligence, and the Defendant's motion to dismiss is hereby denied. The matter will proceed to trial on the designated trial date, unless otherwise ordered by this Court.
