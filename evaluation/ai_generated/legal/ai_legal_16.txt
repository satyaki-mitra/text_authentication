Case Title: Smith v. Johnson

Court: Supreme Court of the State of New York, County of Albany

Docket Number: 2021-CV-012345

Judgment Date: January 15, 2023

Jurisdiction: Civil Court

Parties:
1. Plaintiff: John Smith (hereinafter referred to as "Plaintiff")
2. Defendant: Jane Johnson (hereinafter referred to as "Defendant")

Clause or Case Summary:

In the matter of Smith v. Johnson, the Court found in favor of the Plaintiff, John Smith, on counts of breach of contract and tortious interference with a business relationship. The case arose from an agreement between the parties for the sale of real property, whereby Defendant Jane Johnson agreed to sell her property to Plaintiff John Smith, but subsequently entered into a similar agreement with a third party, thereby breaching the original contract and interfering with Plaintiff's business relationship.

The Court held that Defendant breached the contract by failing to perform her obligations under the sales agreement, and further found that she intentionally and maliciously interfered with Plaintiff's business relationship by entering into a similar agreement with another party, thereby causing harm to the Plaintiff.

As a result of the Court's findings, Defendant Jane Johnson was ordered to pay compensatory damages in the amount of $500,000 to John Smith, as well as reasonable attorney's fees and costs associated with the litigation. The Court further enjoined Defendant from entering into any similar agreements concerning the sale of real property for a period of two years from the date of this judgment.
