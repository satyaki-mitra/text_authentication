Title: Data Processing Agreement - Clause 4: Data Subject Rights

4. Data Subject Rights

4.1 Obligations Regarding Data Subject Requests

4.1.1 Data Processor shall, at the choice of Data Controller, assist Data Controller in fulfilling its obligations under Articles 12 to 22 and Article 34 of the GDPR to respond to requests for exercising the data subject's rights (collectively, "Data Subject Requests"). Data Processor's assistance shall include, but not be limited to, providing reasonable cooperation and assistance necessary for Data Controller to comply with such obligations.

4.1.2 Data Processor shall promptly notify Data Controller if it receives a request from a data subject in relation to this Processing, unless EU Law prohibits such notification on important grounds of public interest.

4.2 Response to Data Subject Requests

4.2.1 Upon receiving a Data Subject Request, Data Processor shall, at the choice of Data Controller:

(a) provide Data Controller with such information and cooperation as is necessary for Data Controller to respond directly to the data subject; or

(b) if requested by Data Controller, take reasonable steps (at Data Controller's expense) to assist in the compliance with the Data Subject Request, including amending, correcting, deleting or restricting the processing of Personal Data.

4.3 Data Processor's Obligations Regarding Access by Data Subjects

4.3.1 Data Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to enable Data Controller to demonstrate compliance with Articles 5(1)(a) to (e) and 25 to 39 of the GDPR, insofar as the processing operations are carried out by Data Processor.

4.3.2 Data Processor shall maintain documentation of all categories of processing activities carried out under this Agreement, including a description of the technical and organizational security measures implemented, and make it available to Data Controller upon request.

4.4 Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach, Data Processor shall without undue delay notify Data Controller thereof, providing all relevant information necessary for Data Controller to comply with its obligations under Articles 33 and 34 of the GDPR.

4.5 Compliance with Laws and Regulations

In performing its obligations under this Clause, Data Processor shall comply with all applicable laws and regulations in the EU Member State in which the Data Processing takes place.

4.6 Liability

Each party's liability arising out of or in connection with this Clause shall be subject to the indemnification and limitation of liability provisions set forth elsewhere in this Agreement.
