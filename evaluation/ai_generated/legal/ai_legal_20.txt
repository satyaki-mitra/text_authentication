Title: Clause Regarding Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure

1. Confidentiality Obligation

Upon entering into this Agreement, the Receiving Party acknowledges and agrees that it shall maintain, during the term of this Agreement and thereafter, the Confidential Information in strict confidence for the sole and exclusive benefit of the Disclosing Party. The Receiving Party shall not disclose any Confidential Information to third parties without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, except as expressly permitted by this Agreement or required by law.

2. Definition of Confidential Information

For the purposes of this Agreement, "Confidential Information" shall mean any technical data, trade secrets, know-how, non-public information, and business or marketing plans disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, either directly or indirectly, in writing, electronically, orally, or by drawings or photographs.

3. Exceptions to Confidentiality Obligation

The obligations set forth in this clause shall not apply to any portion of the Confidential Information that: (a) was rightfully known to the Receiving Party prior to its disclosure by the Disclosing Party without breach of any obligation owed to the Disclosing Party; (b) becomes public knowledge through no fault of the Receiving Party; (c) is lawfully obtained by the Receiving Party from a third party free of any obligation of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed by employees of the Receiving Party who had no access to such information.

4. Remedies for Breach of Confidentiality Obligation

The Disclosing Party shall have the right, in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity, to seek equitable relief (including injunction and specific performance) if the Receiving Party breaches or threatens to breach this clause. The Receiving Party acknowledges that any such breach may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy, and therefore the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief if the Receiving Party breaches or threatens to breach this clause.
