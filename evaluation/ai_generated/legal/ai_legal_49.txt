Title: Data Processing Agreement (DPA) - Controller to Processor Clauses

1. Introduction

1.1 This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between [Controller's Name] ("Controller") and [Processor's Name] ("Processor"), each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

1.2 The Parties have agreed to this DPA in order to comply with their respective obligations under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC ("GDPR").

1.3 This Agreement supplements the main agreement between the Parties governing the Processor's provision of services to the Controller (the "Main Agreement"). In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this DPA and the Main Agreement, this DPA shall prevail to the extent of such conflict or inconsistency.

2. Definitions

2.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA shall have the meanings given to them in the GDPR or the Main Agreement.

3. Processing of Personal Data

3.1 The Controller hereby appoints the Processor as a processor for the processing of personal data on behalf of the Controller, as more particularly described in Schedule 1 (Details of the Data Processing).

4. Compliance with Laws and Regulations

4.1 Each Party shall comply with all laws, regulations, and industry standards applicable to its activities under this Agreement.

4.2 The Processor shall process the personal data only for the purposes set out in Schedule 1 (Details of the Data Processing) and in accordance with the Controller's documented instructions as updated from time to time.

5. Rights and Obligations of the Parties

5.1 The Controller warrants that it has obtained any necessary permissions and authorizations required for the transfer of personal data to the Processor under this Agreement, including any required data protection registrations, notifications, or approvals.

5.2 The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of processing as well as the risks involved for the rights and freedoms of natural persons.

5.3 The Processor shall promptly notify the Controller if it becomes aware of any accidental or unauthorized access to or destruction, loss, or alteration of personal data.
