Title: Data Protection Policy Statement

1. Introduction

1.1 This Data Protection Policy Statement ("Policy") sets out the obligations of [Company Name], a company incorporated in [Jurisdiction] (the "Company"), regarding data protection and the rights of its employees, customers, and business partners ("Data Subjects") in respect of their personal data under EU Regulation 2016/679 General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR").

1.2 The GDPR defines "personal data" as any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

1.3 This Policy applies to all processing of personal data carried out by the Company, its employees, contractors, or agents.

2. Data Protection Principles

The Company complies with the principles of data protection as set out in the GDPR:

2.1 Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency: The Company will only collect and process personal data if it is lawful to do so, transparent about its processing activities, and has the consent of the Data Subject where necessary.

2.2 Purpose limitation: The Company will only collect personal data for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes and not further process in a manner that is incompatible with those purposes.

2.3 Data minimization: The Company will only collect and process the minimum amount of personal data required for its intended purposes.

2.4 Accuracy: The Company will keep personal data accurate and up-to-date.

2.5 Storage limitation: The Company will not store personal data longer than necessary for its intended purposes.

2.6 Integrity and confidentiality: The Company will take appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the integrity and confidentiality of personal data.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing Personal Data

The Company may process personal data on one or more of the following lawful bases:

3.1 Consent: The Data Subject has given clear consent for the processing of his/her personal data for a specific purpose.

3.2 Contractual necessity: The processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the Data Subject is party, or in order to take steps at the request of the Data Subject prior to entering into a contract.

3.3 Legal obligation: The processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the Company is subject.

3.4 Vital interests: The processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of the Data Subject or another person.

3.5 Public task: The processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the Company.
