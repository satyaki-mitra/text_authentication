Title: Data Processing Agreement - Clause 4: Data Subject Rights

4. Data Subject Rights

4.1 Obligations Regarding Data Subject Requests

4.1.1 The Data Processor shall, to the extent legally permitted, promptly notify the Data Controller if it receives a request from a Data Subject for access to, rectification, erasure, or restriction of processing of that Data Subject's Personal Data (hereinafter "Data Subject Request"). The Data Processor shall not respond to such a request without the prior written consent of the Data Controller except where otherwise required by applicable law.

4.1.2 Upon receiving any Data Subject Request, the Data Processor shall cooperate with the Data Controller and take reasonable steps to assist the Data Controller in fulfilling its obligations under this Agreement and any applicable data protection laws, including providing the Data Controller with such information and assistance as is reasonably necessary to respond to the request.

4.2 Response to Data Subject Requests

4.2.1 The Data Processor shall respond to a Data Subject Request promptly and in any event within the timeframe required by applicable data protection laws. The Data Processor's response shall be made directly to the Data Subject, unless local law requires that it be made to the Data Controller.

4.2.2 If the Data Processor is unable to respond fully to a Data Subject Request within the required timeframe, it shall provide the Data Subject with a written explanation setting out the reasons for the delay and the anticipated response time.

4.3 Data Portability

4.3.1 To the extent that the Personal Data is processed by automated means under this Agreement, the Data Processor shall, at the choice of the Data Controller, provide the Data Subject with a copy of that Personal Data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.

4.3.2 The Data Processor may charge a reasonable fee for any such data portability request, based on administrative costs. In no case shall the fee amount to a disproportionate burden on the Data Subject.

4.4 Data Breach Notification

4.4.1 In the event of a data breach, the Data Processor shall comply with its obligations under Clause 7 (Data Security) and promptly notify the Data Controller of any such breach. Such notification shall include all available details regarding the nature of the breach, the categories and numbers of Data Subjects concerned, and the measures taken or proposed to be taken to address the breach and mitigate its potential adverse effects.
