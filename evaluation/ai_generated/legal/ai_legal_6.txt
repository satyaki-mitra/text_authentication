Title: Data Protection Policy Statement

1. Introduction

1.1 This Data Protection Policy Statement ("Policy") sets out the obligations of [Company Name], a company incorporated in [Jurisdiction] (the "Company"), regarding data protection and the rights of individuals ("Data Subjects") in respect of their personal data under EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) ("GDPR").

1.2 The GDPR defines "personal data" as any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

2. The Company's Commitment

2.1 The Company is committed to the protection of personal data and respecting the privacy rights of Data Subjects. The Company will adhere to the principles of GDPR, which include processing personal data lawfully, fairly, and in a transparent manner.

3. Lawful, Fair, and Transparent Processing

3.1 To ensure its processing of personal data is lawful, fair, and transparent, the Company will maintain up-to-date records of all processing activities under its responsibility, including:
   - The purpose(s) for which personal data is processed;
   - The categories of Data Subjects and personal data types;
   - The recipients or categories of recipients to whom the personal data is disclosed;
   - The retention period for storing the personal data;
   - Details of transfers of personal data to third country or international organization.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

4.1 The Company will only process personal data if it has a lawful basis for doing so. The possible lawful bases for processing are:
   - Consent of the Data Subject;
   - Performance of a contract with the Data Subject or to take steps at the request of the Data Subject before entering into a contract;
   - Compliance with a legal obligation to which the Company is subject;
   - Protection of the vital interests of the Data Subject or another person;
   - The Company's legitimate interests, except where such interests are overridden by the fundamental rights and freedoms of the Data Subject.

5. Rights of Data Subjects

5.1 Data Subjects have several rights under GDPR, including:
   - The right to access their personal data;
   - The right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
   - The right to erasure of their personal data (the "right to be forgotten");
   - The right to restrict the processing of their personal data;
   - The right to object to the processing of their personal data; and
   - The right to data portability.
