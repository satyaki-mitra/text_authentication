Title: Data Processing Agreement (DPA) - Clause 7: Data Subject Rights

7. Data Subject Rights

7.1 Obligations Regarding Data Subject Requests

7.1.1 The Data Processor shall, to the extent legally permitted, promptly notify the Data Controller if it receives a request from a Data Subject in relation to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, or objection to the processing of Personal Data.

7.1.2 Upon receipt of such a request, the Data Processor shall cooperate with the Data Controller to facilitate the Data Controller's response to the Data Subject's request in accordance with applicable Data Protection Laws. The Data Processor shall provide reasonable assistance as requested by the Data Controller to enable the Data Controller to comply with its obligations under Data Protection Laws with respect to such requests.

7.2 Response to Data Subject Requests

7.2.1 The Data Processor shall respond directly to any Data Subject request it receives, unless prohibited by applicable law, in which case the Data Processor will notify the Data Controller of such prohibition and provide regular updates on the status of the request.

7.2.2 If the Data Processor responds to a Data Subject request, it shall not disclose to the Data Subject any information that is confidential or proprietary to the Data Controller, unless required by applicable law.

7.3 Data Controller's Responsibilities

7.3.1 The Data Controller shall be responsible for verifying the identity of the Data Subject making a request and for providing necessary documentation or other information reasonably required by the Data Processor to respond to such requests.

7.3.2 The Data Controller shall ensure that its instructions for responding to Data Subject requests are consistent with the Data Controller's obligations under Data Protection Laws and this Agreement.

7.4 Effect of Data Subject Requests on Fees

7.4.1 Any additional costs or fees incurred by the Data Processor in responding to a Data Subject request shall be borne by the Data Controller, unless such costs are due to the Data Controller's failure to comply with its obligations under this Agreement or applicable Data Protection Laws.

7.5 Confidentiality

All parties agree to keep confidential any information received from each other in connection with a Data Subject request, except where disclosure is required by law or pursuant to the terms of this Agreement.

This Clause 7 shall be interpreted and enforced in accordance with the provisions of the Data Protection Laws applicable to each party.
