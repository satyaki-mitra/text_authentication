Case Title: Smith v. Johnson

Court of Jurisdiction: Supreme Court of the State of New York, County of Albany

Docket Number: 2021-CV-123456

Judge Presiding: Honorable Jane Doe

Date Filed: January 1, 2022

Parties Involved:
1. Plaintiff: John Smith (Plaintiff)
2. Defendant: Emily Johnson (Defendant)

Facts:
The Plaintiff, John Smith, alleges that the Defendant, Emily Johnson, breached a contractual agreement between the parties regarding the sale of real property located at 123 Main Street, Albany, New York (the "Property"). Specifically, Mr. Smith asserts that Ms. Johnson failed to transfer ownership of the Property on the agreed-upon closing date of December 31, 2021, and has subsequently refused to complete the sale despite receiving full payment from Mr. Smith.

Claims:
1. Breach of Contract (Count I) - Alleging that Ms. Johnson breached a written agreement for the sale of the Property by failing to transfer ownership on December 31, 2021, and refusing to complete the sale thereafter.
2. Specific Performance (Count II) - Requesting the Court to order Ms. Johnson to transfer ownership of the Property to Mr. Smith as originally agreed in the contract.
3. Damages (Count III) - Seeking compensatory damages for any losses incurred by Mr. Smith due to Ms. Johnson's breach, including but not limited to costs associated with the failed sale and any potential loss of profits from the investment in the Property.
4. Attorney's Fees and Costs (Count IV) - Requesting that the Court award reasonable attorney's fees and costs incurred by Mr. Smith in bringing this action against Ms. Johnson.

Relief Sought:
1. Specific Performance of the contract for the sale of the Property located at 123 Main Street, Albany, New York.
2. Compensatory damages for any losses suffered as a result of Defendant's breach.
3. Reasonable attorney's fees and costs incurred by Plaintiff in bringing this action.
