Title: Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

This Data Processing Addendum ("Addendum") is entered into on the Effective Date by and between [Your Company Name] ("Data Controller" or "Controller"), and [Recipient's Company Name] ("Data Processor" or "Processor"), each a "Party," and together, the "Parties."

WHEREAS:

1. The Parties have entered into an agreement under which the Data Processor agrees to process Personal Data on behalf of the Data Controller (the "Agreement").
2. The European Union's General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("GDPR") and other applicable data protection laws require that the Parties put in place a contract governing such processing to ensure compliance with their respective obligations.
3. The Parties wish to set out their agreement concerning the Processing of Personal Data by the Data Processor on behalf of the Data Controller, as required by Article 28 of the GDPR.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 The terms "Data Subject," "Personal Data," "Processing" (and "Process"), "Controller," "Processor," "Subprocessor," and "Supervisory Authority" shall have the meanings given to them in the GDPR.

1.2 Capitalized terms used but not defined herein shall have the meanings assigned to them in the Agreement.

2. Scope and Duration

2.1 This Addendum applies where and to the extent that the Data Processor is processing Personal Data on behalf of the Data Controller under the Agreement.

2.2 The term of this Addendum shall be co-terminus with the term of the Agreement, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Parties.

3. Instructions

3.1 The Data Controller's instructions for the Processing of Personal Data are set out in the Agreement and any subsequent written instructions given by the Data Controller to the Data Processor from time to time.
