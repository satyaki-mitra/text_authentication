Clause: Indemnification and Hold Harmless Agreement

In this Agreement, the indemnifying party (Indemnifier) agrees to hold harmless and indemnify the indemnified party (Indemnitee), its officers, directors, employees, agents, successors, and assigns against any and all losses, damages, liabilities, claims, actions, judgments, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) that may arise from or in connection with any third-party claim resulting from the Indemnifier's breach of this Agreement, negligence, willful misconduct, or violation of any law or regulation.

Case Summary: United States ex rel. Barko v. Gilead Sciences, Inc., 872 F. Supp. 2d 1156 (N.D. Cal. 2012)

In this case, the plaintiff, a whistleblower under the False Claims Act, alleged that the defendant, Gilead Sciences, Inc., knowingly submitted false claims to federal healthcare programs for its drug Viread, used in the treatment of hepatitis C. The court held that the relator's allegations were sufficient to survive a motion to dismiss, as they contained sufficient factual detail to plausibly suggest that Gilead had violated the False Claims Act by knowingly submitting false claims for Viread. The case proceeded to trial, and Gilead ultimately settled with the government for $250 million.
