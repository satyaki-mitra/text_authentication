Title: Data Processing Agreement - Clause 3: Data Protection Obligations

3.1 Data Controller Obligations

3.1.1 The Data Controller shall, in relation to any Personal Data processed in connection with the Services, comply with all applicable Data Protection Laws and ensure that it has all necessary permissions and notices in place to enable lawful transfer of such Personal Data to the Data Processor for the duration and purposes of this Agreement.

3.1.2 The Data Controller shall have sole responsibility for the accuracy, quality, and legality of Personal Data and the means by which it obtained such data. It shall provide all necessary information relating to the nature of the Personal Data and any special categories of data (if applicable) to the Data Processor upon request.

3.1.3 The Data Controller shall promptly inform the Data Processor if it becomes aware of any accidental or unauthorized access to, or loss, destruction, or alteration of, any Personal Data.

3.2 Data Processor Obligations

3.2.1 The Data Processor shall process Personal Data only in accordance with the documented instructions from the Data Controller (unless required to do so by applicable law), and shall not process such data for any purpose other than the performance of its obligations under this Agreement.

3.2.2 The Data Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including but not limited to: (a) the prevention of unauthorized or unlawful processing of Personal Data; (b) the prevention of accidental loss or destruction of, or damage to, Personal Data; (c) ensuring the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of Personal Data; (d) regular testing, assessing, and evaluating the effectiveness of technical and organizational measures for ensuring the security of the processing.

3.2.3 The Data Processor shall ensure that all personnel who have access to and/or process Personal Data are obligated to keep such data confidential.

3.2.4 Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Data Processor shall securely delete or return all the Personal Data to the Data Controller, unless required by applicable law to store the Personal Data.

3.3 Subprocessors

3.3.1 The Data Processor may engage third-party subprocessors for the processing of Personal Data, provided that: (a) it obtains the prior written consent of the Data Controller; and (b) it imposes data protection terms on any such subprocessor that require them to protect the Personal Data in accordance with this Agreement.
