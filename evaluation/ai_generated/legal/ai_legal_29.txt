Title: Data Protection Policy - Clause 9: Data Breach Notification and Response

9.1 Obligation to Notify

9.1.1 In the event of a data breach, the Data Controller shall promptly notify the Data Subject and the Supervisory Authority in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

9.1.2 A data breach is defined as any unauthorized or unlawful access to, disclosure, loss, destruction, alteration, or acquisition of Personal Data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed by the Data Controller.

9.2 Timeline for Notification

9.2.1 The Data Controller shall provide notification without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than 72 hours after having become aware of a data breach, unless the Data Controller demonstrates that the personal data breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons.

9.3 Content of Notification

9.3.1 The notification shall include the following information:

   (a) A description of the nature of the data breach, including where possible, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects concerned and the categories and approximate number of Personal Data records concerned;

   (b) The name and contact details of the Data Protection Officer or other contact point where more information can be obtained;

   (c) A description of the likely consequences of the data breach;

   (d) A description of the measures taken or proposed to be taken by the Data Controller to address the data breach, including, where appropriate, measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects.

9.4 Obligation to Keep Records

9.4.1 The Data Controller shall maintain a record of all data breaches, whether or not they were communicated to the Data Subject and the Supervisory Authority, in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

9.5 Confidentiality

9.5.1 All parties involved in the notification and response process shall maintain the confidentiality of any information received during the data breach notification and response process.

9.6 Cooperation with Data Subjects

9.6.1 The Data Controller shall provide appropriate assistance to the Data Subject, including providing them with the information necessary to exercise their rights under applicable laws and regulations, such as the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of their Personal Data.

9.7 Cooperation with Supervisory Authority

9.7.1 The Data Controller shall cooperate fully with any investigation by the Supervisory Authority regarding a data breach, providing them with all necessary information and taking all reasonable steps to remedy any identified issues.
