Title: Data Processing Agreement (DPA) - Clause 4 - Data Subject Rights

**Article 4: Data Subject Rights**

1. **Obligations Regarding Data Subject Requests**

   (a) Each Party shall, to the extent required by applicable law, promptly notify the other Party if it receives a request from a Data Subject for access to, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, or objection to the processing of Personal Data. The receiving Party shall not respond to such request without first obtaining the prior written consent of the other Party, unless required by law to do so.

   (b) If a Data Subject exercises any of its rights under applicable data protection laws with respect to Personal Data processed under this Agreement, the Parties agree that they will cooperate and take reasonable steps to assist each other in responding to such request in compliance with their respective obligations under applicable data protection laws.

2. **Cooperation Regarding Access Requests from Supervisory Authorities**

   (a) Each Party shall promptly inform the other Party if it receives a request or communication from a Supervisory Authority relating to the processing of Personal Data under this Agreement. The Parties shall cooperate, on a reasonable basis, to respond to any such requests or communications and provide each other with reasonable assistance necessary to enable either Party to comply with such requests or communications.

   (b) Each Party shall take into account the nature of the processing, the information available to it, and the reasonable cost of providing the requested information to the other Party when responding to a request from a Supervisory Authority.

3. **Data Protection Impact Assessment**

   (a) If either Party is required under applicable data protection laws to conduct a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA), it shall involve the other Party without undue delay, and provide such assistance as is necessary for the DPIA, including sharing relevant information and documentation.

   (b) Each Party shall promptly inform the other Party if it becomes aware of any matters that may give rise to a high risk to the rights and freedoms of Data Subjects in relation to the processing of Personal Data under this Agreement.

4. **Data Protection Officer**

   If either Party appoints a Data Protection Officer (DPO) pursuant to applicable data protection laws, it shall promptly inform the other Party and provide contact details for the DPO. The Parties agree to cooperate with each other and, where appropriate, with the DPOs of each Party regarding their respective obligations under this Agreement and applicable data protection laws.
