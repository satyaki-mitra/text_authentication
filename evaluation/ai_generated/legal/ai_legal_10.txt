Clause: Indemnity and Hold Harmless Agreement

In this agreement, the indemnifying party (Indemnitor) agrees to hold harmless and indemnify the indemnified party (Indemnitee) from any and all actions, claims, damages, liabilities, costs, or expenses (collectively "Claims") arising out of or related to the indemnifying party's acts or omissions in connection with this agreement or the use of the indemnified party's services, products, or properties. The indemnifying party further agrees to reimburse the indemnified party for any payments made by it as a result of such Claims, provided that the indemnifying party is given prompt notice of any such Claim and an opportunity to defend against such Claim at its own expense. This indemnity shall survive termination or expiration of this agreement.

Case Summary: Smith v. Johnson (2021)

In the case of Smith v. Johnson, the plaintiff, Smith, brought a lawsuit against the defendant, Johnson, alleging negligence and breach of contract in connection with a real estate transaction. Specifically, Smith claimed that Johnson had failed to disclose material defects in the property and had engaged in fraudulent activities during the sale process. The court found in favor of Johnson, holding that the indemnity clause contained in the purchase agreement between the parties provided Johnson with protection against any Claims arising out of or related to the transaction, including those based on negligence or breach of contract. The court further held that Smith had failed to provide Johnson with prompt notice of the alleged defects and had waived his right to claim indemnity by not allowing Johnson the opportunity to defend against the Claim at its own expense as required by the agreement.
