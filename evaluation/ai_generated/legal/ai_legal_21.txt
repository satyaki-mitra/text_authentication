Title: Data Processing Agreement (DPA) Clause

1. Introduction

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between the Data Controller ("Controller") and the Data Processor ("Processor"), each a "Party" and together the "Parties." This DPA supplements the existing agreement(s) between the Parties (the "Agreement"). The purpose of this DPA is to comply with the obligations imposed on Controllers and Processors under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (General Data Protection Regulation or "GDPR").

2. Definitions

For the purposes of this DPA, the terms set out in Annex A shall have the meanings assigned to them therein. Capitalized terms not otherwise defined herein shall have the meaning given to them in the Agreement.

3. Processing of Personal Data

The Processor shall process the Personal Data solely for the purposes and within the scope described in the Agreement, and in accordance with the Controller's documented instructions as updated from time to time (the "Purpose"). The Processor shall not process the Personal Data for any purpose other than the Purpose unless required by applicable law, in which case the Processor shall inform the Controller of such requirement before processing unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

4. Compliance with Laws and Regulations

The Processor shall process the Personal Data in compliance with all applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards, including but not limited to the GDPR. The Processor shall ensure that its personnel engaged in the processing activities are informed of the confidential nature of the Personal Data and have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

5. Security Measures

The Processor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure or access, in particular where the processing involves the transmission of data over a network, and against all other unlawful forms of processing. The specific technical and organizational measures implemented by the Processor are described in Annex B.

6. Data Subject Rights

Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Processor shall assist the Controller by implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as this is possible, for the fulfillment of the Controller's obligation to respond to requests for exercising the data subject's rights laid down in Chapter III of the GDPR.
