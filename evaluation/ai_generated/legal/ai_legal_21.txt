Case Title: Smith v. Johnson

Court of Appeals, State of New York

Case No: A-2021-6789

Date: March 15, 2023

Judgment

In the matter of Smith v. Johnson, the Court of Appeals of the State of New York has rendered its decision.

Facts:
The plaintiff, Mr. Smith, initiated this action against the defendant, Ms. Johnson, alleging breach of contract concerning a real estate transaction. The parties entered into a written agreement on June 1, 2021, whereby Ms. Johnson agreed to sell her property to Mr. Smith for a purchase price of $500,000. Subsequently, Ms. Johnson failed to transfer the deed of the property to Mr. Smith by the contractually stipulated closing date of August 31, 2021.

Issues:
1. Did Ms. Johnson breach the contract by failing to transfer the deed of the property to Mr. Smith by the agreed closing date?
2. If so, is Mr. Smith entitled to damages as a result of the breach?

Held:
Upon careful consideration of the facts and applicable law, the Court finds that Ms. Johnson did indeed breach the contract by failing to transfer the deed of the property to Mr. Smith by the agreed closing date. As a direct consequence of this breach, Mr. Smith suffered damages in the form of additional costs associated with the delay, including but not limited to title insurance and legal fees.

Decision:
The Court grants judgment in favor of Mr. Smith and against Ms. Johnson for the sum of $10,000 as compensatory damages for the losses directly resulting from the breach of contract. It is further ordered that Ms. Johnson shall transfer the deed of the property to Mr. Smith within 30 days of this judgment being served upon her.

This judgment constitutes the final decision in this matter and is binding on all parties.
