Case Title: Smith v. Johnson

Court: Supreme Court of the State of New York, County of XYZ

Case No.: ABC-123456

Date: January 1, 20XX

Judgment:

In the matter between John Doe Smith, Plaintiff, and Jane Doe Johnson, Defendant, the Court hereby issues the following judgment:

WHEREAS, this action arises out of a dispute concerning breach of contract in regards to the sale of real property located at 123 Main Street, Anytown, USA (the "Property") between the Plaintiff and the Defendant;

WHEREAS, on March 15, 20XX, a written agreement of sale was executed by both parties, wherein the Defendant agreed to sell and convey the Property to the Plaintiff for the sum of $500,000 (the "Purchase Price");

WHEREAS, the Plaintiff has alleged that the Defendant failed to comply with the terms of the agreement, specifically by failing to transfer clear title to the Property;

WHEREAS, on June 30, 20XX, the Plaintiff notified the Defendant of their intention to terminate the agreement and seek damages for breach;

WHEREAS, the Plaintiff has provided evidence that demonstrates the Defendant's inability to transfer clear title due to outstanding liens against the Property;

WHEREAS, the Defendant has failed to provide a valid defense or counterclaim in response to the Plaintiff's allegations;

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS ORDERED THAT:

1. The Defendant is hereby found guilty of breach of contract as defined by Section 14(a) of the General Obligations Law of the State of New York.
2. The Plaintiff shall be awarded actual damages in the amount of $50,000, representing the costs incurred to investigate and remedy the Defendant's failure to transfer clear title to the Property.
3. The Defendant is hereby ordered to pay the Plaintiff the sum of $50,000 within 45 days from the date of this judgment.
