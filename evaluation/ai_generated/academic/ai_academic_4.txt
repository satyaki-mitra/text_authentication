Title: Investigating the Impact of Climate Change on Coral Reef Biodiversity: A Comparative Analysis of Caribbean and Indo-Pacific Regions

Abstract

Background Context: Climate change, characterized by rising sea temperatures, ocean acidification, and increased frequency of extreme weather events, poses a significant threat to the global coral reef ecosystem. This study aims to compare the resilience of coral reef biodiversity in two distinct regions—the Caribbean and Indo-Pacific—under the influence of climate change.

Research Methodology: A comprehensive, multi-disciplinary approach was employed, integrating data from various sources including long-term monitoring programs, satellite imagery, and underwater surveys. The study utilized statistical analysis to compare the changes in coral cover, species richness, and community structure between the two regions over a 30-year period (1985-2015). Additionally, the impact of local anthropogenic pressures was controlled for by considering data from protected and unprotected areas within each region.

Key Findings: The results revealed a significant decline in coral cover across both regions, with the Indo-Pacific experiencing a less severe decrease compared to the Caribbean. However, the Caribbean maintained a higher species richness and exhibited a more resilient community structure, suggesting a potential adaptation strategy to climate change. The study also identified a positive correlation between protection status and coral cover, underscoring the importance of marine protected areas in preserving coral reef biodiversity.

Implications: This research provides valuable insights into the differential responses of coral reef ecosystems to climate change in the Caribbean and Indo-Pacific regions. The findings highlight the need for targeted conservation strategies, with a focus on enhancing protection measures and promoting adaptive management practices in vulnerable areas. Furthermore, the study emphasizes the importance of cross-regional collaboration in global efforts aimed at preserving coral reef biodiversity under the looming threat of climate change.

Keywords: Climate Change, Coral Reefs, Biodiversity, Caribbean, Indo-Pacific, Resilience, Conservation
