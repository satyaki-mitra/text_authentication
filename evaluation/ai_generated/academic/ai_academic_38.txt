Title: Investigating the Impact of Climate Change on the Reproductive Success of the Endangered Leatherback Turtle (Dermochelys coriacea) in Pacific Coastal Regions

Abstract

Background Context: The Leatherback Turtle (Dermochelys coriacea), a critically endangered marine species, is facing significant threats due to climate change and human activities. This study aimed to assess the impact of oceanic temperature variations on the reproductive success of leatherback turtles nesting in Pacific coastal regions.

Research Methodology: A long-term field study was conducted over a period of nine years (2012-2020) at three key nesting sites along the Pacific Coast of Costa Rica, namely Tortuguero National Park, Ostional Wildlife Refuge, and Las Baulas National Marine Park. Data collection involved daily monitoring of turtle nesting activities, measurement of clutch size, incubation duration, and hatchling emergence success. Additionally, temperature logs were placed within the nests to record the microclimate conditions during incubation. Statistical analyses were performed using generalized linear models (GLMs) to determine the relationship between oceanic temperatures and reproductive parameters.

Key Findings: The results indicated a significant negative correlation between oceanic surface temperature anomalies and clutch size (r=-0.72, p<0.01), hatchling emergence success (r=-0.68, p<0.01), and incubation duration (r=0.59, p<0.01). Specifically, an increase in oceanic surface temperature by 1°C was associated with a decrease in clutch size by 24%, hatchling emergence success by 27%, and an extension of incubation duration by 6%.

Implications: The findings suggest that climate change is adversely affecting the reproductive success of leatherback turtles nesting in Pacific coastal regions. This study provides critical evidence to support conservation efforts aimed at protecting this endangered species from the detrimental effects of global warming. Further research is necessary to investigate potential adaptation strategies for leatherback turtles and to explore mitigation measures that could alleviate the impacts of climate change on their reproductive success.

Keywords: Leatherback Turtle, Dermochelys coriacea, Climate Change, Reproductive Success, Pacific Coast, Costa Rica, Global Warming, Conservation, Adaptation, Mitigation.
