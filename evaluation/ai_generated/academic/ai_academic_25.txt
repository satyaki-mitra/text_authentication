Title: Investigating the Impact of Environmental Enrichment on Cognitive Development in Rodents: A Systematic Review

Abstract

Background Context: The exploration of environmental enrichment (EE) as a means to stimulate cognitive development in rodents has garnered substantial attention within the scientific community. EE, characterized by the provision of complex and stimulating living conditions, has been posited to enhance various aspects of cognition, including learning, memory, and problem-solving abilities. This systematic review aims to synthesize existing literature on the influence of environmental enrichment on cognitive development in rodents, providing a comprehensive overview of current findings and implications for future research.

Research Methodology: A comprehensive search was conducted across several databases, including PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, and PsycINFO, employing keywords such as "environmental enrichment," "rodent," "cognitive development," "learning," "memory," and "problem-solving." Studies published between 1980 and 2021 were considered for inclusion in this review. The selected studies underwent a rigorous screening process, with those meeting the predefined eligibility criteria being subjected to quality assessment using the ARRIVE (Animal Research: Reporting of In Vivo Experiments) guidelines.

Key Findings: The findings of this systematic review indicate that environmental enrichment significantly enhances cognitive development in rodents across various domains. Enriched environments have been shown to improve learning and memory performance in tasks such as Morris water maze, novel object recognition, and passive avoidance tests. Moreover, EE has been found to facilitate problem-solving abilities, as evidenced by improved performance on tasks like the radial arm maze and Y-maze. The positive effects of EE were observed across several species of rodents, including rats, mice, and hamsters.

Implications: The findings of this systematic review underscore the potential of environmental enrichment as a valuable tool for promoting cognitive development in rodents. By providing complex and stimulating living conditions, researchers can facilitate improvements in learning, memory, and problem-solving abilities. These findings have significant implications for understanding the neural mechanisms underlying cognitive development and may inform the design of more effective interventions aimed at enhancing cognitive function. Furthermore, this review highlights the importance of standardized methodology and quality assessment in animal research to ensure the validity and reliability of findings.

In conclusion, this systematic review provides a comprehensive synthesis of existing literature on the impact of environmental enrichment on cognitive development in rodents. The findings suggest that EE significantly enhances learning, memory, and problem-solving abilities across various species. These insights have implications for both basic research into cognitive development and the design of interventions aimed at improving cognitive function.
