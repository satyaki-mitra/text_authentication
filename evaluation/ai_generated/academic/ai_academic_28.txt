Title: An Examination of the Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Global Economic Development: A Comparative Analysis of Developed and Developing Countries

Abstract:

This study delves into an intricate exploration of the transformative influence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on global economic development, focusing on the contrasting perspectives of developed and developing countries. The research aims to elucidate the multifaceted interplay between AI adoption, economic growth, and socio-economic disparities, offering novel insights into the potential for AI to catalyze sustainable development across the globe.

The investigation commences with a comprehensive review of the existing literature on AI and its economic implications, underscoring the burgeoning interest in understanding this cutting-edge technology's role in shaping the future of global economies. The study then proceeds by employing a mixed-methods approach to gather data from both qualitative and quantitative sources. Primary data was gathered through surveys and interviews with key stakeholders, including policymakers, business leaders, and AI researchers, while secondary data was sourced from international economic databases, industry reports, and government publications.

The analysis is structured around three central themes: (1) AI's potential for productivity enhancement, innovation, and job creation; (2) the barriers to AI adoption, particularly in developing countries, including infrastructure deficiencies, data privacy concerns, and regulatory challenges; and (3) the socio-economic implications of AI, such as the widening digital divide, changes in labor markets, and ethical considerations.

The findings reveal that developed countries are more likely to benefit from AI due to their advanced technological infrastructure, abundant data resources, and conducive regulatory environments. In contrast, developing countries face numerous obstacles in leveraging AI for economic growth, including limited access to capital, human capital deficits, and insufficient digital literacy. However, the study also highlights that developing countries can seize opportunities presented by AI by focusing on strategic policy interventions aimed at addressing these challenges.

The study concludes by offering recommendations for policymakers in both developed and developing countries to maximize the benefits of AI while mitigating potential risks. These include fostering a supportive regulatory environment, investing in digital infrastructure, promoting digital literacy and skills development, and prioritizing collaborative efforts between the public and private sectors.
