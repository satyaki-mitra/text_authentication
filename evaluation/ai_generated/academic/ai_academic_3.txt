Title: Exploring the Impact of Climate Change on Phytoplankton Diversity in the Northeast Pacific Ocean: A Multi-decadal Analysis

Abstract

Background Context: The Northeast Pacific Ocean, a vital biogeochemical hotspot, has experienced significant climate change over the past few decades. This alteration in environmental conditions has potentially influenced the diversity and distribution of phytoplankton, primary producers that underpin marine food webs. Understanding these changes is crucial for predicting future ecosystem dynamics and managing marine resources sustainably.

Research Methodology: We conducted a multi-decadal analysis (1950-2018) using satellite ocean color data to examine temporal and spatial variations in phytoplankton diversity across the Northeast Pacific Ocean. The study employed the Modified Shannon Diversity Index as a measure of phytoplankton diversity, while environmental variables such as sea surface temperature (SST), chlorophyll-a concentration (Chl-a), and primary productivity were extracted from the same dataset for correlation analysis. A generalized additive model (GAM) was employed to assess the relationship between climate change indicators (SST and Chl-a) and phytoplankton diversity.

Key Findings: Our findings revealed a significant decline in phytoplankton diversity over the study period, with a more pronounced decrease observed since the 1980s. The GAM analysis indicated that both SST and Chl-a were positively associated with phytoplankton diversity, suggesting that warming temperatures and increased nutrient availability may stimulate diversity in the short term but could lead to detrimental effects in the long run due to eutrophication and alterations in species composition.

Implications: The observed decline in phytoplankton diversity has potential implications for marine food webs, fisheries productivity, and carbon cycling. Our findings highlight the need for further research on the ecological consequences of changing phytoplankton communities and emphasize the importance of incorporating climate change considerations into management strategies for sustainable marine resource utilization in the Northeast Pacific Ocean. Future studies should focus on investigating the mechanisms underlying these observed changes, as well as examining potential mitigation strategies to promote resilience in the face of ongoing climate change.
